# Am I a Soldier of the Cross


**Date/source note:** 1724

## Background

1724. Watch, stand fast in the faith. Be brave, be strong. First Corinthians chapter 16
verse 13. This Isaac watchs him appeared after most of his others, not in a collection
of hymns, but in a published volume of his sermons. It followed a sermon entitled Holy
Fortitude based on 1 Corinthians chapter 16 verse 13. Watch, stand fast in the faith. Be
brave, be strong. Over a century later this song played a role in American hymnology
bringing together the powerful gospel team of evangelist D.L. Moody and soloist Iris
Sanky.

It happened this way. By the 1870s D.L. Moody had become a world-famed evangelist, but
he badly needed a musician to lead singing at his meetings. On a Saturday night in 1870
he preached at a convention in Indianapolis. At the last minute a tax collector named
Iris Sanky was asked to lead singing. After the service Moody assaulted Sanky with
questions. Where are you from? Are you married? What is your business? Sanky replied
that he lived in Pennsylvania, was married, had two children, and worked for the
government, where upon Moody said abruptly, you will have to give that up.

Sanky dumbfounded asked, What for? To help me in my work I have been looking for you for
the last eight years. The next day Sanky received a card from Moody suggesting they meet
on a certain corner that evening at six. Sanky arrived first. When Moody showed up he
said nothing but entered a nearby store for a large box. He asked Sanky to stand on the
box and sang. Sanky dutifully hoisted himself up and sang Isaac Watts,
