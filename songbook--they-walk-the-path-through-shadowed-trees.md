# They walk the path through shadowed trees,

SONG:

They walk the path through shadowed trees,
Where dangers hide, no birds, no breeze.
A roar breaks out, a beast appears,
But courage fades, and reveals his fears.

The woods grow thick, the path is rough,
Yellow bricks fade under leaves and dust.
With each step forward, hearts beat fast,
‘Cause deep growls echo, unknown, they pass.
Toto walks close, Dorothy feels the strain,
But Tin Man says, “We’ll be safe again.”

They walk the path through shadowed trees,
