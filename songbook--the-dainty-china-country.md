# The Dainty China Country

SONG:

The Dainty China Country

in a land of china, smooth and white,
Dorothy and friends climbed high to sight—
over the wall, they saw it clear,
a dainty land, strange and near.
houses, barns, and animals too,
all made of china, shining through.
milkmaids, princes, clowns with cracks,
a perfect world with fragile backs.

oh, be careful where you stand,
in this dainty china land.
one wrong step, one wrong touch,
and things won’t ever be as much.

the milkmaid’s cow gave a kick,
china pail broke, it was quick—
"look what you’ve done!" she said in pain,
"my cow's leg snapped, won’t walk again."
Dorothy apologized, stepped back slow,
afraid to cause more hurt below.
they walked on, careful not to break,
more of the lives that were at stake.

oh, be careful where you stand,
in this dainty china land.
one wrong step, one wrong touch,
and things won’t ever be as much.

a china princess, dressed in gold,
warned Dorothy of a truth untold,
“if I run and fall, I’ll crack,
and never be as beautiful back.”
Mr. Joker, with his cracks,
showed the scars, so many tracks.
“better to live, free to roam,
than be placed on a shelf alone.”

oh, be careful where you stand,
in this dainty china land.
one wrong step, one wrong touch,
and things won’t ever be as much.

they reached the wall on the other side,
the lion jumped, a church died.
china shattered, pieces fell,
“we’re lucky,” Dorothy said, “farewell.”
the brittle world, a fragile scene,
wasn’t meant for hands so keen.
with a crack and a clatter, they moved along,
to lands where strength and heart belong.

oh, be careful where you stand,
in this dainty china land.
one wrong step, one wrong touch,
and things won’t ever be as much.
