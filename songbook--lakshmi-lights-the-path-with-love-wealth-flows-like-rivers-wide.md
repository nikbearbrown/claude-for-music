# Lakshmi lights the path with love, wealth flows like rivers wide,

SONG:
Lakshmi lights the path with love, wealth flows like rivers wide,
Golden glows and lotus blooms, with blessings she’ll provide.
She’s the dawn, the day, the star, a spark in every night,
Lakshmi’s grace, it fills the space, brings the world to life.

Lakshmi, bringer of light, hands full of fortune and peace,
She sits upon her lotus throne, where abundance never cease.
Fields and rivers blessed by her touch, petals soft as daybreak,
She’s the spark in the darkest dark, the love that makes hearts awake.

Embodied in charm and grace, she shields from all that’s harm,
