# They walk with courage through fields of fears,

SONG:

They walk with courage through fields of fears,
where mice and lions, paths, and tears
dance beneath the yellow sky,
in the land where hope and shadows lie.

A growl, a flash, teeth laid bare,
the Wildcat leaps, slicing the air.
Woodman swings, his axe agleam,
cuts through silence, splits the dream.
