# The Wonderful City of Oz

SONG:

The Wonderful City of Oz


Through green glass eyes, they wander and roam,
streets paved in emeralds, a jewel-built home.
Green glows the sky, green stones in a row,
in a city where only green dreams can grow.

It’s the color of wonders and fears alike,
we’re lost in the emerald, where dreams take flight.
In the city of green, the Great Oz hides,
and we’re seeking for truths beyond these skies.

Green lemonade, green shoes on their feet,
every face turned away from the Lion they meet.
Pushing green carts through a world so bright,
no beasts or horses in this city of light.

It’s the color of wonders and fears alike,
we’re lost in the emerald, where dreams take flight.
In the city of green, the Great Oz hides,
and we’re seeking for truths beyond these skies.
