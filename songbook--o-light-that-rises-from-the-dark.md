# O light that rises from the dark,

SONG:

[
]
O light that rises from the dark,
In this night, I pray,
Your face, a fleeting star, still haunts me,
A flash of warmth in the chill of Boston’s wind.
You walked through my life, a breath in the storm,
And now, I wait, unsure if you'll return.
Fall to the ground, I fall to my knees,
My prayer, a whisper that fills the heart,
For the dawn breaks, soft as a promise,
But promises fade like footsteps in the sand.

[Chorus]
O light that rises from the dark,
In this night, I remember.
I fall to the earth, I fall to my knees,
For my prayer is born in silence.
If the dawn can break again, if you can hear me still,
Will your voice return, or will you disappear?
