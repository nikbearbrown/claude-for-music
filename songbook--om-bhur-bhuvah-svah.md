Tat Savitur Varenyam
Bhargo Devasya Dheemahi
Dhiyo Yo Nah Prachodayat.

In the fields of green where the mountains rise,
We gather 'round with our hearts open wide,
To sing a song that's ancient and bright,
Om Bhur Bhuvah Svah, transcending the light.
