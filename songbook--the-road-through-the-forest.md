# The Road Through the Forest

SONG:

The Road Through the Forest

she walks (& the yellow brick) road
breaking beneath her steps—stumbling, yet steady—
the path (uneven), holes where she steps aside,
where he falls without mind to the ground,
(no care at all)

they walk where yellow bricks break,
rough, uneven, each step they take.
Scarecrow stumbles, but he doesn’t mind,
with Dorothy there, they leave troubles behind.

she laughs, picks him up, dusts him off,
fields grow (gray) in lonesome tones,
fewer trees, stones, just sky & empty earth—
they walk alone, where Toto jumps, she swerves,
but he falls—twisting, curved.

“no hunger here,” he says,
“straw in my head—nothing to lose,”
(painted mouth, a blue eye)
“if i had a mouth to chew,
my thoughts would spill, i’d come undone.”

they walk on, shadows bend in the sun,
the trees close in, thick & slow,
sky disappears, (a darkened glow).

the land grows lonesome, no fences to be found,
the farther they go, the grayer the ground.
she offers him bread, but he shakes his head,
“no hunger, just straw instead.”
she talks of Kansas, gray & dry,
“why leave Oz, beneath its vibrant sky?”

her hands clutch this empty dream,
as night folds around them
a cottage stands, hidden in green.

they walk where yellow bricks break,
rough & uneven, each step they take.
Scarecrow stumbles, yet doesn’t mind,
with Dorothy, they leave troubles behind.

they move (onward) through the night,
where steps break, trees close, shadows flow.
the trees grow thick, light fades low,
darkness wraps the path they know.

“i see a cottage,” Scarecrow says,
“a bed of leaves to rest your head.”
she lies down, finds her sleep,
as he stands guard, his watch to keep.

so they move through night
where yellow bricks break
rough and uneven, each step they take.
Scarecrow stumbles, yet he doesn’t mind,
with Dorothy there, they leave troubles behind.

& still they move,
with dreams in her hands,
while he stands with painted eyes,
the straw beneath, the shadows flow,
through trees thick and paths unknown.
