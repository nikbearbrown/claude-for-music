# Chapter 8: The Queen Beneath the Paws

SONG COMMANDS (duet):

Chapter 8: The Queen Beneath the Paws
Styles: Soulful, Gospel, Bass, Trap, Female Baritone, Cello, Diatonic harmonica, Four-string tenor banjo, Jumbo guitar, Stroh violin acoustic country, emo

[Male Voice – Spoken Word, low and steady]
We walked into woods where the shadows crept
And danger stirred where silence slept
A growl from the dark, a flash of fur
And suddenly no one dared to stir

[Female Voice – Lyrical, slow rhythm]
A beast came near with a rumbling chest
Eyes like fire, no time to rest
But the lion, brave, with trembling paws
Stood up tall and roared with cause

[Male Voice – Rhythmic ballad tone]
He faced the beast, his mane held wide
Not to fight, but protect with pride
The others watched, their hearts so tight
As roars became the voice of right

[Female Voice – Whispery, hopeful tone]
The forest hushed, the danger passed
But the test had come and gone so fast
And just beyond the curling tree
A tiny voice spoke urgently

[Male Voice – Gentle spoken flow]
A mouse in a crown, so small, so sure
With a royal tone and heart so pure
“My people are trapped,” she softly pled
“Beneath the roots where the wild have fed”

[Female Voice – Soft, lullaby cadence]
So they knelt beside her silken throne
And promised she would not be alone
The lion bent down with mighty grace
To save the tiny, trembling race

[Male Voice – Sung with harmonica between lines]
He pulled and pawed, he cleared the way
The beasts were gone by end of day
And the Queen bowed low, her voice sincere
“You’ll have our help when need draws near”

[Female Voice – Sung with violin swelling]
They learned that strength is not just size
But measured in who lifts and tries
And even kings with tooth and claw
Can kneel before the smallest law

[Together – Harmonized, rising gently]
So onward went the heart and hoof
With every step, a living proof
That kindness rules through all it sees
Even whispered soft beneath the trees
