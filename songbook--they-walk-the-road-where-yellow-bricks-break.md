# They walk the road, where yellow bricks break,

SONG:

They walk the road, where yellow bricks break,
Rough and uneven, each step they take.
Scarecrow stumbles, yet he doesn’t mind,
With Dorothy there, they leave troubles behind.

Scarecrow stumbles on yellow stone,
Missing bricks, they walk alone.
Toto jumps and Dorothy swerves,
But he falls full-length, all twists and curves.
She picks him up, they laugh it off,
Together in the dust, where the road gets rough.

They walk the road, where yellow bricks break,
Rough and uneven, each step they take.
Scarecrow stumbles, yet he doesn’t mind,
With Dorothy there, they leave troubles behind.
