# The Frogs Who Wanted a King

SONG:

[Verse - Male Solo, whimsical storytelling]
The frogs all lived in a lily-pad town
Where nobody bossed anybody around
They splashed in the lake and they blinked at the sky
Till one little frog gave a government sigh

[Verse - Male Solo, curious and bouncy]
"We need us a king with a crown and a rule
Who'll sit on a throne by the side of the pool
A king would be grand, and a king would be neat
A king would make muddy old frog life complete"

[Verse - Male Solo, spoken-word bounce]
So they hollered to Jupiter high in the blue
"Please send us a king who knows what to do"
Jupiter chuckled and scratched at his chin
Then tossed down a log with a thunderous spin

[Chorus - Male Solo, harmonica lift]
Be careful what crown you call from the sky
Some kings just sit and some kings fly by
A quiet old log may give you no thrill
But a hungry new king has a very long bill

[Verse - Male Solo, soft comic fear]
That log hit the lake with a WHOMP and a SPLAT
And every frog yelled, "What kind of king's that"
They dove to the bottom and hid in the goo
Where the bubbles said blub and the tadpoles said boo

[Verse - Male Solo, brighter and silly]
But one brave frog with a wobble-knee hop
Peeked at the king from a lily-pad top
The king did not wiggle, or croak, or complain
He simply lay floating again and again

[Verse - Male Solo, mischievous rhythm]
Soon frogs were climbing all over his back
They tickled his bark and they called him a hack
"This king is a nothing, a bump, and a stick
Let's ask for a ruler with style and a kick"

[Chorus - Male Solo, stronger folk groove]
Be careful what crown you call from the sky
Some kings just sit and some kings fly by
A quiet old log may give you no thrill
But a hungry new king has a very long bill

[Verse - Male Solo, darker playful tone]
So Jupiter frowned at their ribbity fuss
"You want to be ruled, do you, every last one of us"
He sent down a stork with a royal long stride
And the frogs clapped their webby hands side by side

[Verse - Male Solo, dramatic hush]
"Oh look how he walks with his beak in the air"
"He certainly seems like a monarch with flair"
But the stork bent low with a terrible gulp
And swallowed one frog like a green little pulp

[Bridge - Male Solo, low and cautionary]
Then gulp went another
And gulp went some more
The lake became quiet
From shore to shore

[Verse - Male Solo, slow and mournful]
The oldest frog cried, "If we only had been-"
But the stork snapped him up before "content" could begin
And breakfast was frogs, and dinner was too
And supper was somebody's uncle named Lou

[Chorus - Male Solo, full warm warning]
Be careful what crown you call from the sky
Some kings just sit and some kings fly by
A quiet old log may give you no thrill
But a hungry new king has a very long bill

[Outro - Male Solo, fading hum]
So splash while you're free in your comfortable spot
A boring old peace may be better than not
For frogs who want kings just to feel more grand
May end up as lunch in a long-beaked land
