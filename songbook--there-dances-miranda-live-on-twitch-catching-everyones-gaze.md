# There dances Miranda, live on Twitch, catching everyone's gaze.

SONG:

In a room lit by screens, in a chair that's seen better days,
There dances Miranda, live on Twitch, catching everyone's gaze.
She'd shuffle and shuffle, with a grin so wide,
And viewers tuned in to watch her joyful ride.

She’s not too rehearsed or overly styled,
But in her own way, she's perfectly wild.
With every level up, and each victory big or little,
She'd laugh and say, “Time for a happy dance jiggle.”
