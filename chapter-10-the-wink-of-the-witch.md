# Chapter 10: The Wink of the Witch

SONG COMMANDS (poem):

Chapter 10: The Wink of the Witch
Styles: Lullaby, Raspy, Bluegrass, Emo, Hip Hop, Five-string banjo, Chromatic harmonica, Viola, Semi-hollow electric guitar, Baritone guitar, emo, folk, violin, harmonica

[Male Voice – Spoken Word, steady and foreboding]
We left the gates with a whisper behind
A promise in green, a hope redefined
But to earn what we seek, we had to go
Where the Wicked Witch lets nothing grow

She watched from the hills with a single eye
And sent her wolves to make us die
But I stood tall, with axe in hand
And met the danger where it would land

[Male Voice – Melodic flow, quiet strength]
Then crows came dark, a shadow swarm
But straw fought back with clever form
The scarecrow moved with grace and spin
And brought down birds with a stitched-in grin

[Male Voice – Lower register, tense and firm]
Bees flew next in a vengeful swarm
But our tin man rose like a metal storm
He stood his ground, without a cry
And let no sting slip safely by

[Male Voice – Harmonica break, breath of resolve]
She tried once more with magic's spark
A wind, a twist, the sky went dark
But lions roar where fear would flee
And courage means “you still chose me”

[Male Voice – Softened, thoughtful close]
So we came to her castle, shadowed and still
With shoes on fire and iron will
She watches with hate, with tricks and charm
But even her power can lose its harm

Because we are four, and truth holds weight
And together we walk through every gate
