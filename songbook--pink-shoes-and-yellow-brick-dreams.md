Pink Shoes and Yellow Brick Dreams (

Adventures of Bear and Parvati I

She walks with grace upon a path,
With pink shoes on, no fear, no wrath.
A beauty born of eastern lands,
With dreams too large to hold in hands.
She steps through shadows, shadows fold,
A heart of light, a spirit bold.
In every stride, a story told,
A yearning deep, a wish of gold.

Beside her walks a giant soul,
A man of code, yet wholly whole.
Tall as oak, and strong as steel,
With hands that build and hearts that heal.
A poet's mind, a warrior’s will,
But in her gaze, he’s quiet, still.
Their steps align, a perfect rhyme,
Two travelers in borrowed time.
