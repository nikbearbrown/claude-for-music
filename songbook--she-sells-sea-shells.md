# She sells sea-shells

SONG:

She sells sea-shells


She sells sea-shells on the sea-shore,
With a basket of treasures and so much more.
The shells she sells are sea-shells,I’m sure,
Glistening gems of the ocean's floor.

Sea-shells by the sea,such wonders to explore,
She sells sea-shells,oh what a lovely chore!
On the sandy shore where the waves gently roar,
She sells sea-shells,treasures to adore.

For if she sells sea-shells on the sea-shore,
Then surely she finds what the sea has in store.
A conch,a whelk,and a starfish so bright,
Each shell a memory of the ocean’s light.

Sea-shells by the sea,such wonders to explore,
She sells sea-shells,oh what a lovely chore!
On the sandy shore where the waves gently roar,
She sells sea-shells,treasures to adore.
