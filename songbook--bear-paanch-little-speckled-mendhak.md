# Paanch Little Speckled Mendhak

SONG:

A Bilingual Counting Song (Hindi + English)

[Baritone Male Voice - Playful and Gentle]
Paanch little speckled mendhak
Sat on a speckled lakdi
Eating some most delicious keeda
Yum yum!
One did koodna into the talaab
Where the paani was nice and thanda
Then there were chaar green speckled mendhak
Glug glug!

[Baritone Male Voice - Learning Echo]
Paanch means five
Mendhak means frog
Lakdi means log

[Baritone Male Voice - Playful and Gentle]

Yum yum!

[Baritone Male Voice - Learning Echo]
Chaar means four
Keeda means bug
Talaab means pool

[Baritone Male Voice - Playful and Gentle]

[Baritone Male Voice - Bright and Bouncy]
Oh no more speckled mendhak
Not one on the lakdi
Each one took a dive
Now they're swimming feeling alive
Down in the talaab - oh how they thrive!
Splish splash!

[Baritone Male Voice - Joyful and Lyrical]
The talaab is full of mendhak
No more on the log
They're khush in the paani now
Where they belong!
They croak a joyful tune
Beneath the chand and moon
Singing ribbit ribbit with a happy swoon!
Ribbit ribbit!

[Baritone Male Voice - Cheerful Chorus]
Yum yum keeda in the air
Snapping snacks without a care
Glug glug! A bellyful treat
Swimming 'round with sticky feet!

Splish splash they koodna and play
Ribbit ribbit! Night and day!
Khush little mendhak sing

Everything feels right

[Baritone Male Voice - Final Chorus]
Yum yum keeda
Glug glug paani
Splish splash talaab
Ribbit ribbit mendhak

Paanch, chaar, teen, do, ek
Count the frogs and take a peek
No more logs, just poolside cheer
Froggies singing loud and clear!

Yum yum! Glug glug!
They hop and hug
Splish splash! In the bubbly bath
Ribbit ribbit! Hear them laugh!
