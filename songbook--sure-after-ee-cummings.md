# sure (after ee cummings)

SONG:

sure (after ee cummings)

small dreams rise in crooked lines
where little hands can conjure cows
wooden painted brown of truth
in fields of standing still they graze

he laughs the rich men into seas
and pockets sheep and tricks with ease
simple hearts may dance on ground
where little hopes in circles round

(oh would it be, for all our dreams
to wander forth in crooked seams)
