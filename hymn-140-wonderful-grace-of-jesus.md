# Wonderful Grace of Jesus


**Date/source note:** 1918

## Background

1918. Through Him we have received grace. Romans 1. 5. A. Worship leaders around the
world owe a debt of gratitude to Lillianus publishing company, which was started by the
author of this hymn, Hall-Dor Lillianus. He was born in the Theor District of Norway in
1885 and as a child, emigrated to the United States with his family. From his youth,
Hall-Dor had a musical bent, a talent that served him well when he entered the ministry
in 1910 in the newly established Church of the Nazarene. Between his sermons and visits,
Hall-Dor took musical studies by correspondence and soon began writing gospel songs.

In 1919 he published his first book. At three years later he organized his own
publishing house in Indianapolis, Indiana, where he was serving as pastor of the first
Church of the Nazarene. For the next decade, Hall-Dor preached, traveled, wrote hymns,
and published songbooks. In 1930 the Nazarene publishing house in Kansas City, Missouri
became interested in expanding its fledgling efforts in the field of music. They agreed
to purchase Lillianus publishing company, provided that Hall-Dor himself would move to
Kansas City and manage it.

He remained in that role until he retired in 1950, then served in an advisory capacity
until his death in Aspen, Colorado in 1959. Today, Lillianus publishing company is one
of the largest church music publishers in the world, owning more than 20,000 song
copyrights
