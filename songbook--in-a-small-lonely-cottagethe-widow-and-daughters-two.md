# In a small lonely cottage,the widow and daughters two,

SONG:

In a small lonely cottage,the widow and daughters two,
Snow-White and Rose-Red,in a world crisp and new.
Like roses they bloomed,one white and one red,
Together they thrived,from the same tender bed.

Kindness and courage,in tales that are told,
Lead to adventures,and blessings untold.
Keep true to your heart,let your virtues unfold,
And watch as your life turns the dullest days gold.

Through forests they wandered,where wild creatures play,
No harm ever found them,in night or in day.
An angel watched over,from close or from far,
Under the glow of the evening’s first star.

Kindness and courage,in tales that are told,
Lead to adventures,and blessings untold.
Keep true to your heart,let your virtues unfold,
And watch as your life turns the dullest days gold.

Then hardship arrived,in the form of a bear,
At their door in the cold,seeking warmth from his lair.
With hearts open wide,they let fear aside,
And found a friend true,not a foe to deride.

Kindness and courage,in tales that are told,
Lead to adventures,and blessings untold.
Keep true to your heart,let your virtues unfold,
And watch as your life turns the dullest days gold.
