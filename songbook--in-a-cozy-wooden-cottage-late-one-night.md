# In a cozy wooden cottage, late one night,

SONG:

In a cozy wooden cottage, late one night,
A woodman sighed with his pipe alight,
"Without a child, it’s far too still,
To fill this home with laughter’s thrill."

The wife replied with a wistful smile,
“Oh, if we had one, even tiny and small,
As little as my thumb, I’d still feel grand,
To hold such a child within my hand.”

Tom Thumb, Tom Thumb, as small as can be,
Brave as a lion and wild as the sea,
Though tiny in size, his spirit would roam,
For a heart so bold, no place but home.

So it happened, and lo and behold,
Tom Thumb was born—a sight to hold!
Though little he stayed, his eyes shone bright,
A clever spark, so quick and light.

“Put me in the horse’s ear!” he said,
“Guide you through fields with words instead!”
