"Give Peace a Chance (And Peas Too)"

We walk through gates of green and gold,
Emerald walls where stories hold,
A hum of wonder, voices bold—
Now we’re back, our dreams retold.

(Chorus)
Give peace a chance, hear the call,
Give peas a chance, there's room for all.
No more war, no more lies,
Let’s find the truth behind the guise.

We came for hope, for something fair,
With dreams to lift us through despair.
But all we find is smoke and air—
Can the mighty ones even care?
