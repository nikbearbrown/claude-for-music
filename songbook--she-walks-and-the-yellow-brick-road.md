# she walks (& the yellow brick) road

SONG:

she walks (& the yellow brick) road
breaks beneath
her steps—stumbling, yet steady—
the path (uneven)
holes where her feet avoid, & where he falls
with no mind to the ground,
(no care at all)
They walk the road, where yellow bricks break,
Rough and uneven, each step they take.
Scarecrow stumbles, yet he doesn’t mind,
With Dorothy there, they leave troubles behind.
& she—laughing, picks him up again,
brushing off the dust
where fields grow (gray) in lonesome tones,
and fewer trees, fewer stones,
just this empty earth & sky
Scarecrow stumbles on yellow stone,
Missing bricks, they walk alone.
Toto jumps and Dorothy swerves,
But he falls full-length, all twists and curves.
She picks him up, they laugh it off,
Together in the dust, where the road gets rough.
“no hunger here,” he says,
“just straw in my head—nothing to lose,”
(painted mouth, a blue eye)
“if i had a mouth to chew,
my thoughts would spill, i’d come undone.”
They walk the road, where yellow bricks break,
Rough and uneven, each step they take.
Scarecrow stumbles, yet he doesn’t mind,
With Dorothy there, they leave troubles behind.
hey walk on, two shadows bent in the fading sun,
the trees close, breathing thick & slow,
the sky disappears (a darkened glow)
