# The Deadly Poppy Field

SONG:

The Deadly Poppy Field

They walked through fields where poppies grow,
Bright red blooms with a dangerous glow.
With hope for Oz, but sleep draws near,
In the flowers’ grip, they face their fear.

Morning breaks by the river wide,
Peaches and plums by Dorothy’s side.
They push the raft through the rushing stream,
But the current’s swift, and they lose the dream.
Scarecrow caught by a pole in the flow,
Left behind, they drift below.

They walked through fields where poppies grow,
Bright red blooms with a dangerous glow.
With hope for Oz, but sleep draws near,
In the flowers’ grip, they face their fear.

Lion leaps, pulls them ashore,
They find Scarecrow, stranded no more.
A stork’s kind wings lift him high,
Brings him back, beneath blue sky.
They journey on with dreams in stride,
Till poppies bloom on every side.

They walked through fields where poppies grow,
Bright red blooms with a dangerous glow.
With hope for Oz, but sleep draws near,
In the flowers’ grip, they face their fear.
