# Dust and Dreams – Chapter 5: The Roar Behind the Tremble

SONG COMMANDS (duet tongue lang portuguese):

Dust and Dreams – Chapter 5: The Roar Behind the Tremble
Styles: Spoken Word, Country Melodic Acoustic, Deep Bass/Male Baritone, Finger Snaps, House, Ukulele banjo, Orchestral harmonica, Piccolo violin, Archtop guitar, Resonator guitar, emo, violin, whistling

[Spoken Word Intro – Soft, poetic delivery with light ambient chords beneath]

Let’s learn some Portuguese vocabulary in the shade of the trees
Where fear walks quiet on a trembling breeze
It’s not about roars or the way you fight
But the choice to stay when you want to take flight

This language is courage in lyrical form
Each line a shelter from inner storm
So listen for more than the growl or the sound
Because even the bravest can fall to the ground

From “coragem” to “medo”, from “leão” to “voz”
We learn that fear doesn't mean we’re lost
It’s the step through the dark, not the one that flees
That teaches a heart how to live with ease

[Vocal Warm-Up Duet]
Doh me soh
Oooooo aaaaaa mmmmmm

[TONGUE TWISTER – Male Voice Spoken Word]
Lions leapt lightly, leaving leafy lanes
Lurking low in laughter and lyrical refrains
Loud little roars looped in looping chains!

[Female Voice – Curious, steady]
Ooooooh...
We walked through grass that rose like waves
With nothing but silence beneath the caves
Then a roar came fierce, a flash of mane
A lion leapt from the forest chain

[Male Voice – Shaky but strong]
Rugi pra assustar, mas meu medo ficou
No fundo dos olhos, minha força falhou
“Não sou valente,” o leão confessou
“Mas quero coragem, só isso eu sou”

[Female Voice – Gentle empathy]
He trembled like leaves on a windblown tree
Ashamed of his fear for the world to see
But I touched his paw and said, “It’s alright—
Courage is standing in spite of fright”

[Male Voice – Warm, humbled tone]
“Se posso andar mesmo com medo em mim
Talvez já seja corajoso, sim”
Com passos firmes, nos juntamos aos três
E a estrada ganhou mais um talvez

[Female Voice – Uplifting]
So now we’re four, not just one or two
Each of us lost, but finding what's true
A girl, a scarecrow, a tin man, a friend
And a lion who learns where the brave begin

[Male Voice – Whistling undertone, soft violin]
Caminhamos juntos, sem hesitar
Com medo, sim—mas vamos tentar
Pois coragem não grita, nem sempre se vê
Mas vive no passo de quem diz “por quê?”

[Together – Harmonized duet, resonant and gentle]
So we kept on down the yellow road
Each heart unburdened by what it showed
Pois mesmo quem treme pode ser herói
Se não fugir quando tudo destrói

Let’s learn some words.
Vamos aprender algumas palavras.

English	Portuguese
Lion is	Leão
Roar is	Rugido
Fear is	Medo
Courage is	Coragem
Friend is	Amigo / Amiga
Brave is	Valente
Walk is	Caminhar
Grass is	Grama
Forest is	Floresta
Cry is	Chorar
Voice is	Voz
Try is	Tentar
Strength is	Força
Step is	Passo
