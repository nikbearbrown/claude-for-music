# FUNDEVOGEL

SONG:

FUNDEVOGEL

[Shel Silverstein Style]
[Verse 1]
The forester walked through the whispering trees,
Till a baby’s cry rode the autumn breeze.
Up, up, on a branch so high,
A little lost bird with a baby’s sigh.

[Verse 2]
He took the child with gentle hands,
Named him Fundevogel, through fate’s demands.
Lina laughed, “We’ll always play!”
They grew like roots in the same soft clay.

[Verse 3]
But hush, old Sanna stirs the pot,
She mutters dark and plots a plot.
“Boil the boy, and make him stew,”
But Lina whispers, “We’ll slip right through.”

[Verse 4]
So hand in hand, through morning’s hush,
They ran and ran through thorns and brush.
