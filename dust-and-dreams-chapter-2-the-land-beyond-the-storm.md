# Dust and Dreams – Chapter 2: The Land Beyond the Storm

SONG COMMANDS (duet lang portuguese):
Dust and Dreams – Chapter 2: The Land Beyond the Storm

Styles: spoken word, Gospel, Acoustic Country/R&B, Aggressive Delivery, Baritone, Clackers, Polyphonia harmonica, Hybrid banjos, Steel guitar, Parlor guitar, Double bass violin, whistling

[Spoken Word Intro – Soft, poetic delivery with light ambient chords beneath]

Let’s learn some Portuguese vocabulary with a girl from the plains
Not from textbooks or drills that circle like trains
But through melody, story, and memory refrains
Where a house takes flight and a twister unchains

These aren’t just words—they’re footsteps and skies
Tijolos amarelos where adventure lies
Each rhyme is a clue, each verse is a key
To open a world where language runs free

So follow the path, with your ear and your heart
We’ll learn as we sing—every line plays a part
From "bruxa" to "magia", from "vento" to grace
Let rhythm and rhyme take grammar’s place
