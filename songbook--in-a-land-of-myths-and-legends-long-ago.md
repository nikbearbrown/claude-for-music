# In a land of myths and legends, long ago,

SONG:


In a land of myths and legends, long ago,
Lived Rama, a hero with love aglow,
For Sita, his maiden, fair and true,
Their love, a bond the heavens knew.

Oh, Rama’s love for Sita, shining bright,
Through trials, they held on tight,
A love as strong as mountains high,
A love that will never die.

Their love was tested; Sita was lost,
Taken by a demon at great cost,
But Rama’s love was unwavering,
He searched with friends, hope never wavering.

Across lands and over seas,
Rama journeyed with hope and pleas,
Through forests dark, valleys deep,
A promise to Sita, he swore to keep.

Oh, Rama’s love, a guiding light,
Through darkest night, an endless fight,
Unyielding love, through every plight,
Rama's love, a beacon bright.

In battle's heart, amidst fire and storm,
Rama’s courage stood, unwavering, warm,
Against evil’s might, he conquered all,
For love of Sita, standing tall.

Back to the kingdom, peace restored,
Together forever, their spirits soared,
A love eternal, beyond time’s measure,
Rama and Sita, an immortal treasure.
