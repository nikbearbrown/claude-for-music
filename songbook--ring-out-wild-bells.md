# Ring Out, Wild Bells

SONG:

Ring Out, Wild Bells
(Adapted into Song Lyrics)

[Verse 1]
Ring out, wild bells, to the wild sky,
Let the clouds and frosty light fly by.
The year is fading, shadows grow,
Ring out the old, let it go, let it go.

[Chorus] (Soulful Choir with Chimes & Bells)
Oh, ring out, ring out, wild bells tonight,
Bring the dawn, a brand-new light.
Ring in hope, and love, and peace,
A song of joy that never will cease.
