# Adventures of Pinocchio II

SONG:

Adventures of Pinocchio II

[Verse 1]
In a dusty room of wood and glue,
Old Mastro Cherry, tired and blue,
Carved from knots a life anew,
A secret stirring, unknown to view.

[Verse 2]
In shuffled Geppetto, wig like corn,
Of jokes and teases he was worn,
“Polendina!”—a name like thorn,
Yet from it grew a dream reborn.

[Verse 3]
“I’ll craft a boy who leaps and spins,
Who dances wild with childlike grins,
A puppet free from woes or sins,
And travels where the world begins.”

[Verse 4]
Mastro handed over wood so fine,
But it sprang free, as if by design!
A fateful twist, a sudden sign—
Was life within that pine?

[Verse 5]
“Take care!” cried Geppetto in a huff,
“For gifts like yours can be quite rough!”
Yet hand in hand, they sealed enough,
With dreams both bold and tough.

[Verse 6]
With wood in arms, Geppetto strode,
Homeward down the twilight road,
With hope and mischief’s heavy load,
To chisel dreams into a code.

[Verse 7]
And from that wood, a boy would spring,
Who’d dance and leap and dare to sing.
To carve a soul—a daring thing!
