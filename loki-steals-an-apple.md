# Loki Steals an Apple

---

## 1. Plain Lyrics (DistroKid)

In a land where the gods
Sip eternity’s brew
Lived Idunn with apples of gold,it’s true
These weren’t your ordinary apples,but ones that keep
All the gods young,strong,and never weak
One day Loki,the trickster sly
Got in a scuffle,oh me,oh my!
His mischief brewed a fearsome storm
And from Asgard’s walls,he’d soon be torn
Through his antics,so bold and brash

Idunn was snatched in a lightning flash
Thiazi,a giant with a fierce,cold stare
Took her away,through the icy air
Fly high,fly true,brave falcon in the blue
Rescue the keeper of youth,make the old anew
In the dance of giants and
Gods,where shadows weave
Find the light,find the apples,and never leave
But Loki,you see,couldn’t let this be
For without the apples,no immortality

So he turned to a falcon,swift and keen
To rescue Idunn,unseen,serene
Back to Asgard,they did fly
With Thiazi chasing through the sky
But the gods were ready,their weapons drawn
And Thiazi was defeated,gone by dawn
Fly high,fly true,brave falcon in the blue
Rescue the keeper of youth,make the old anew
In the dance of giants and
Gods,where shadows weave

Find the light,find the apples,and never leave
His daughter Skadi,with eyes so cold
Came seeking vengeance,bold and told
But the gods are clever,they made a pact
With marriage and laughter,they’d enact
Skadi’s heart was won with charm
And Njord’s hand,which brought no harm
In the end,peace was found
And Idunn’s apples,safe and sound
Fly high,fly true,brave falcon in the blue

Rescue the keeper of youth,make the old anew
In the dance of giants and
Gods,where shadows weave
Find the light,find the apples,and never leave
So if you find an apple,golden and sweet
Think of the tales where gods
And giants meet
For every fruit has a story ripe
In the orchard of gods,bold and type
Fly high,fly true,brave falcon in the blue

Rescue the keeper of youth,make the old anew
In the dance of giants and
Gods,where shadows weave
Find the light,find the apples,and never leave

**Tempo:** ~92 BPM

**Feel:** Mournful, sparse, building to cathartic release
**Style:** gospel, folk hymn, soul, Chicago blues, gospel protest

| Bridge | 4 | Textural shift and emotional lift |
| Outro | 4 | Fade with ambient tail and final refrain energy |

---

**Instrument List:**
- Organ: harmonic foundation and transitional fills
- Choir: harmony support and chorus lift
- Acoustic Guitar: primary rhythm bed and song movement
- Electric Guitar: primary rhythm bed and song movement
- Handclaps: pulse, groove, and dynamic movement

---

**Section-by-Section Notes:**

**Intro:** Keep the warm-up intimate and centered, with the lead instrument setting the harmonic bed.
**Verse 1:** Keep accompaniment sparse so the text stays intelligible; favor close, conversational vocal delivery.
**Chorus:** Add harmonies, broaden the stereo image, and let the rhythm section support the emotional peak.
**Bridge:** Shift texture with a held chord, solo response, or reduced percussion before returning to the main feel.
**Outro:** Let the final phrase breathe, then fade with reverb or natural ambience.

---

**Production Notes:**
Build the track around lyric clarity first, then add color in small layers. Keep the mix spacious enough for the vocal to carry the story, with the strongest dynamic lift reserved for the chorus or final refrain. Use the inferred style blend as a palette rather than a rigid genre box.
