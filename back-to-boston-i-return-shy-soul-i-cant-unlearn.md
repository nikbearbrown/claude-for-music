# Back to Boston, I return, shy soul I can’t unlearn,

SONG:

Back to Boston, I return, shy soul I can’t unlearn,
From UCLA to Northeastern halls, the weight of a heart yearn,
I think of her, that beauty, Indian eyes that shine so bright,
But I never spoke, never moved, now she fades into the night.

Do the bugs feel heartbreak as they crawl the ground?
Do the birds lose hope when no bond is found?
I can only wait and pray, 'neath this Boston sky,
That heartbreak will fade, like wings that fly.

Ladybug strolls on leaves so green, tiny gem, unseen,
Bringing joy where it roams, silent in its dream.
Does it ache, like I do now, when it loses track?
Or does it find peace, knowing one day it’ll come back?
