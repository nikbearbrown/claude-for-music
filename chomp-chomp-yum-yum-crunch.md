# 🎵 “Chomp-Chomp Yum Yum Crunch!

SONG:

🎵 “Chomp-Chomp Yum Yum Crunch!”
Styles: bouncy ukulele, hand percussion, acoustic folk-pop, kids choir, melodic humming, raspy talk-rap bridges, upbeat claps and whistle

🎵 “Chomp-Chomp Yum Yum Crunch!”

[Intro – All Voices with Whistling + Claps]
Do–mi–so, here we go!
Mmmmmmmm...
Chomp-chomp... yum, yum... crunch!
Snack time’s here, let’s munch-munch-munch!

[Verse 1 – Female Voice, playful rhythm]
Tomato bugs with olive heads
March across your sandwich bread
They say it loud, they say it proud:
Chomp-chomp... yum, yum... crunch! 🍅🐞
Eat your veggies all in a bunch.

[Verse 2 – Male Voice, raspy groove with stomps]
Leafy dog with lettuce hair
Wags his tail in veggie flair
He barks real loud from his leafy lunch:
Chomp-chomp... yum, yum... crunch! 🐶🥬
Fruits and veggies they're great to munch!

[Chorus – All Voices + Handclaps + Kids echo]
Chomp-chomp... yum, yum... crunch!
Take a bite, you’ve got a bunch
Fruits and veggies, loud and proud
We chew, we laugh, we sing it loud!
Chomp-chomp... yum, yum... crunch!
Eat your veggies all in a bunch.

[Verse 3 – Female Voice, dreamy melody]

Chomp-chomp... yum, yum... crunch! 🐢🥝🍋
Fruits and veggies they're great to munch!

[Verse 4 – Male Voice, quick talk-rap with beat snaps]

Chomp-chomp... yum, yum... crunch! 🦈🍓
Eat your veggies all in a bunch.

[Bridge – Kids Call & Response]

Chomp-chomp... yum, yum... crunch!
Fruits and veggies they're great to munch!

[Verse 5 – Female Voice, harmony layering in]

Chomp-chomp... yum, yum... crunch! 🐩🥦
Eat your veggies all in a bunch.

[Final Chorus – All Voices, upbeat with layered whistles]
Chomp-chomp... yum, yum... crunch!

Fruit and veggies can’t be beat!
Chomp-chomp... yum, yum... crunch!
(Make your tummy do a happy punch!)
