# Now Thank We All Our God


**Date/source note:** 1636

## Background

1636. In everything give thanks, for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus for you, 1
Thessalonians chapter 5 verse 18. An old English preacher once said, a grateful mind is
a great mind, and the Bible agrees. There are 138 passages of Scripture on the subject
of thanksgiving, and some of them are powerfully worded. Colossians chapter 3 verse 17
says, and whatever you do in word or deed, do all in the name of the Lord Jesus, giving
thanks to God the Father through Him. 1 Thessalonians chapter 5 verse 18 adds, in
everything give thanks, for this is the will of God in Christ Jesus for you.
