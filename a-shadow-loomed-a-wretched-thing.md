# A shadow loomed, a wretched thing,

SONG:

[Verse]
A shadow loomed, a wretched thing,
With pleading voice, yet dark within.
"No solace mine, no hand to hold,
No warmth to banish endless cold."

[Verse]
"You gave me life but left me cursed,
A thing despised, by fate reversed.
Yet grant me one of kindred frame,
To share my grief, to share my shame."

[Verse]
I turned away, my heart in strife,
Could I create another life?
Another wretch to haunt the earth,
To mock the stars that saw my birth?

[Verse]
His voice grew fierce, his visage wild,
His words no longer meek nor mild.
"If love I gain not, fear shall reign,
I’ll shroud your world in death and pain!"

[Verse]
Yet still he knelt, his rage now hushed,
A fire dimmed, a tempest crushed.
"A mate," he vowed, "no more, no less,
To flee this world of hate's excess."

[Verse]
I heard his plea, yet fear took hold,
