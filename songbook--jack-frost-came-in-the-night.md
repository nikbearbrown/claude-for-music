# jack frost came in the night

SONG:

jack frost came in the night
(the world—all gleaming) white,
painting windows, roofs (and trees)
snowflakes dance (they whisper) breeze.
Jack Frost came in the night,
Left the world all gleaming white,
Painted windows, roofs, and trees,
Snowflakes dancing in the breeze.
jack frost (oh, so sly)
whispers winter’s lullaby
with a brush of icy blue
the world is fresh (and new).
Jack Frost, Jack Frost, oh so sly,
Whispered winter’s lullaby.
With his brush of icy blue,
He makes the world seem fresh and new.
Earth's Winter Dress
earth wears her winter dress
(pure and still and white)
a snowy silence falls
across the hills—so quiet, so bright.
a sparkling cloak, so grand, so wide
(nature’s magic, awake inside).
Earth has put her winter dress,
Pure and white, and motionless,
Snowflakes cover field and hill,
Silent, beautiful, and still.
a sparkling cloak, so grand, so wide
(nature’s magic, awake inside).
Earth's winter gown, oh so grand,
A snowy quilt across the land.
In her white and sparkling cloak,
Nature’s magic has awoke.
