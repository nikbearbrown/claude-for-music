A THOUSAND NIGHTS AND A NIGHT

A Thousand Nights and a Night

[Verse 1]
In the name of Allah, who reigns above,
With blessings on prophets sent with love.
Of tales and wonders, past and bright,
Comes the Thousand Nights and a Night.

[Verse 2]
In India’s lands, by China’s shore,
A king ruled just, beloved and more.
Shahryar the elder, wise and grand,
And Shah Zaman ruled Samarcand.

[Verse 3]
Twenty years they ruled, hand in hand,
Each a lord over sea and land.
Then Shahryar longed to see his kin,
To reunite with his younger twin.

[Verse 4]
But fate would twist and fate would turn,
In courtly halls where secrets burn.
Betrayal struck, hearts turned to stone,
Each king left feeling cold and alone.

[Verse 5]
They saw in gardens, slaves and queens,
Indulgence woven through hidden scenes.
“Curse them all!” both brothers swore,
For trust in love, they had no more.

[Verse 6]
Thus rode they forth, in sorrow’s flight,
Through deserts pale and star-strewn night.
Until at last they met a bride,
Whose story strange would turn the tide.
