# The Fiend’s Vow

SONG:

The Fiend’s Vow
A Poem in the Style of Mary Wollstonecraft Shelley

[Verse 1]
The moon arose, a ghostly eye,
Upon the waves so vast and deep.
Within my hands the tools did lie,
To forge a soul I dared not keep.
A second curse, a sister shade,
A creature wrought from mortal crime—
Would she, too, be unafraid,
And scorn his form in loathing time?

[Verse 2]
He watched, a specter at the pane,
His lips curled tight in mocking glee.
A life unformed, a bond profane,
A fate too dark to let it be.
With trembling hands, in frenzied flight,
I tore apart what once had been—
And cast it down, denied the night,
Denied the birth of kindred sin.

A scream rang sharp upon the air,
A howling, wretched, fiendish cry.
