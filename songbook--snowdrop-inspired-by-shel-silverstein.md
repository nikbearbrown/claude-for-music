# Snowdrop (Inspired by Shel Silverstein)

SONG:

Snowdrop (Inspired by Shel Silverstein)

Once upon a snowy day,
A queen sat stitching red and gray.
Three drops of blood fell down below,
She wished for a daughter white as snow.

Snowdrop grew, fair as can be,
But her stepmother frowned, and jealousy
Turned love to hate, joy to rage,
And Snowdrop’s story turned a page.

A servant led her to the woods so deep,
But his heart was kind, and Snowdrop’s keep
Was left with dwarfs, who saved her fate,
While her stepmother plotted at every gate.

Once as a peddler, once with a comb,
Each time the dwarfs brought Snowdrop home.
At last, an apple sealed her sleep,
