# Nearer, My God, to Thee


**Date/source note:** 1840

## Background

Thirteen years later, while rummaging through a stack of old papers, Elizabeth found
this unfinished poem. As she re-read it, it didn't impress her as being very good, but
when George insisted she finish it, she dutifully scribbled an ending in pencil. He
printed a few copies, one of which landed on the desk of musician William Howard Don in
Cincinnati, who set the verses to music, and published it in his songs of devotion. To
love Christ more, Elizabeth once said, is the deepest need, the constant cry of my soul.
Out in the woods and on my bed and out driving, when I am happy and busy and when I am
sad and idle.

The whisper keeps going up from more love. More love. More love.
