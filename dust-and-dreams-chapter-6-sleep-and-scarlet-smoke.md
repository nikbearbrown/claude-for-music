Vamos aprender algumas palavras.

English	Portuguese

Sleep is	Sono
Rest is	Descanso
Friend is	Amigo / Amiga
Help is	Ajudar
Dream is	Sonho
Field is	Campo
Walk is	Caminhar
Soft is	Suave
Lift is	Levantar
Danger is	Perigo
Wake up is	Acordar
