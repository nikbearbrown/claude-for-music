# The Light of Forbidden Art

SONG:


The Light of Forbidden Art
(A Mary Shelley Poem)

[Verse 1]
The night lay thick with secrets old,
Beneath the stars so dim and cold.
I traced the path where reason led,
Through vaults where science knelt to dread.

[Verse 2]
A voice within, both bold and dire,
Called forth the spark, the kindling fire.
Through books of wisdom, dark and vast,
I sought the key to shun the past.
