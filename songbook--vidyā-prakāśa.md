# Vidyā Prakāśa

SONG:


Vidyā Prakāśa
Light of Knowledge

Om Gam Ganapataye Namaha
Vighnāni Vināshaya Mārgam Viśodhaya
Lord Ganesha, hear my call; sweep away all barriers that stand tall.

Vidyā Prakāśa Jyoti
Jñāna Deepam Hridaye
Asato Mā Sadgamaya
Tamaso Mā Jyotirgamaya
Knowledge shines like morning sun, wisdom burns within my heart;
Guide me from illusion's veil, into truth's illumined art.

Om Svādhyāya Namaha
Pūrna Vidyā Prāptaye
Nava Mārga Darśanam
Om Shanti Shanti Shanti
Blessed be the journey learned, chapter closed and wisdom earned;
New horizons dawn today, peaceful heart to light the way.

Mrityor Mā Amritam Gamaya
Bhaya Tyāga Sāhasam
Gayatri Jyoti Prabhā
Dhiyo Yo Nah Prachodayāt
From what ends to what's eternal, fear released for courage gained;
Sacred light now guides my thoughts, mind inspired and unrestrained.

Punah Punah Nava Jīvanam
Vidyālaya Samāpanam
Karma Yoga Pāthikā
Nava Srishti Ārambha
Once more life renews its dance, school days end as new roads start;
Work becomes my sacred path, creating worlds with mind and heart.

Om Tryambakam Yajāmahe
Bandhanān Mukti Dehi Me
Gam Ganapataye Vijayāya
Om Shanti Shanti Shanti
To the wise one we give praise, free these chains that bind my days;
Victory to new beginnings now, peaceful heart in all my ways.

Sarvam Jñānam Prapūrnam
Sampūrnam Astu Jīvanam
Let all knowledge be complete, as life's journey stands fulfilled.
