# Rama’s Love for Sita

SONG:

Rama’s Love for Sita
In the lands where ancient rivers sing,
And stories float on sacred wing,
There bloomed a love, as bright as dawn,
Between a prince, his heart withdrawn.
Rama’s soul met Sita’s grace,
And found in her a warm embrace.

Their love, a beacon, pure and strong,
A melody, a sacred song.
Through trials fierce and endless night,
They clung to love, their shared light.
