# A Letter from Home

SONG:


A Letter from Home
(A Mary Shelley Poem)

[Verse 1]
A trembling hand, a faded page,
Soft words that cross the distance wide,
A voice so dear calls through the haze,
From mountain's heart and lakes beside.

[Verse 2]
Oh, sweet Geneva, time stands still,
Beneath your sky, my soul remains.
Though wandering far, I hear you still,
Your rivers singing through my veins.
