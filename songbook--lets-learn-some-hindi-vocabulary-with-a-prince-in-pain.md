# Let’s learn some Hindi vocabulary with a prince in pain

SONG:

🎵 The Ramayana of Valmiki – Chapter 3: Rama’s Exile to the Forest
Styles: acoustic country/folk, soulful gospel, raspy vocals, violin, tabla, harmonica, humming, bluegrass banjo, whistling

[Spoken Word Intro – Female Voice, wistful and steady with harmonium and soft guitar arpeggios]
Let’s learn some Hindi vocabulary with a prince in pain
Whose truth walked barefoot through storm and rain
This isn’t a tale of golden thrones
But of forest paths and broken stones

No chalk, no desk, no quiz to take
Just Sita’s vow and Lakshman’s ache
From “vanvaas” to “tyaag”, from “ashru” to “vachan”
In every goodbye, a new word’s begun

[Vocal Warm-Up – Duet]
Ray doh lah
Mmmmmm ooooooo aaaaahhhhhh

[Tongue Twister – Male Voice Spoken Word]
Rama rode roads where rivers ran red
Righteous resolve replacing royal bed
Rani and rishi walked where few had tread

[Male Voice – Raspy, deep tone with tabla and violin]
Dashrath wept, his crown grew dim
For truth meant letting go of him
Ram bowed down, his eyes like flame
To duty’s call, he gave his name

[Female Voice – Gentle, bittersweet with humming and harmonica]
He left behind the silken floor
Sita stepped barefoot through the door
Her love not gold, not feast or fame
But walking beside him just the same

[Male Voice – Quiet strength, soft banjo picking]
Lakshman stood with folded hands
“Where you go, I also stand”
No palace walls, just forest wide
No need for thrones when love’s inside

[Female Voice – Lilting with layered violin swells]
Sita ne pehna ban ka vastra
Uske prem mein tha poora sansaar
Ram ne kaha, “Main chal raha hoon”
Sita boli, “Main bhi saath hoon”

[Together – Harmonized Duet, mellow and moving]
So they walked through trees and ancient skies
With dharma written in their eyes
