# The Search for the Wicked Witch

SONG:

The Search for the Wicked Witch

hey left behind (emerald) gates where
shadows grow—
fields of green lost in whispers (westward)
where no paths go

They left the Emerald gates behind,
no path to find the Witch, no way to mind.
Through fields of green to lands unknown,
they headed west, where shadows are grown.

against the wind we go blind—searching
for the wicked, hiding
in the place where sun dips low—
there lies a terrible she who knows

wolves like shadows leap, silver
whistles pierce, her breath
summons the night (tin man’s axe
singing sharp) against the beasts,
fur and teeth in piles of sleep
