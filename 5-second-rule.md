5 - Second Rule

Nik Bear vocalizes one of his dining experiences at Stetson Dining Hall at Northeastern.

Vocalization, Lyrics, Arrangement, and Production: Nik Bear Brown

At Stetson Dining Hall, where I eat,
It slipped from my fingers, a cookie so sweet,
Flew off the countertop, landed near my feet.
I stared at it lying, a moment of doubt,
Five seconds to decide, before someone shouts out.

Oh, cookie, my delight, now you're free to roam,
But if I pick you up, would anyone know?
Would they turn their heads or pass me by,
Or maybe just wonder why I’d even try?
