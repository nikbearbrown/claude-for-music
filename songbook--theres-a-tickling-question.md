# there’s a tickling (question)

SONG:


there’s a tickling (question)
whispered far & wide
about a little woodchuck, (what wood might he abide)
given the chance (with his paw) & gaze so steady—
how much wood would he toss, if he could
(hold & let fall)
how much wood would a woodchuck chuck
if a woodchuck could chuck wood
how much wood could a woodchuck chuck
(if a woodchuck could chuck wood)
(as much as he could) he’d stack it as he should
a forest’s secret (if only he would)
how much wood would a woodchuck chuck
if a woodchuck could chuck wood
would he chuck from dawn ‘til dusk’s last sigh
or perhaps stack it neat (under sky so high)
maybe a castle (with towers grand)
all from the timber in his small woodland
how much wood would a woodchuck chuck
if a woodchuck could chuck wood
imagine the forest, the chuckling of feet
a shuffling, a flicking where wood shadows meet
how much wood would he toss into breeze
(with a tail’s flicker) if he so pleased
how much wood would a woodchuck chuck
if a woodchuck could chuck wood
how much wood could a woodchuck chuck
(if a woodchuck could chuck wood)
as much as he might, & all that he could
(in the manner only woodchucks would)
how much wood would a woodchuck chuck
if a woodchuck could chuck wood
so ponder the woodchuck, in twilight’s glow
tossing his timber (row after row)
for he chucks what he can (and all that he should)
in the secretive way only woodchucks could
how much wood would a woodchuck chuck
if a woodchuck could chuck wood
how much wood would a woodchuck chuck
(if a woodchuck could chuck wood)
as much as he could, & chuck as he should
(in the way only woodchucks would)
how much wood would a woodchuck chuck
if a woodchuck could chuck wood
think of him chucking, when next you ponder
his feet & his tail—(his wood-stacked wonder)
for he’d chuck as much wood as he could
& chuck only (as a woodchuck should)
how much wood would a woodchuck chuck
if a woodchuck could chuck wood
