# Lullaby and Goodnight

SONG:

Lullaby and Goodnight
With roses bedight
Baby's wee bed,
Lay thee down and rest,
May thy slumber be blessed.

Lullaby and goodnight,
Let the angels beside,
Keep you safe every day,
In the soft moonlight,
Thy mother's delight.

Close your eyes, dear,
With no fear,
Let the night be sweet,
Dreams ever so neat.

Sleep tight, little star,
With dreams afar,
Moonbeams whisper low,
