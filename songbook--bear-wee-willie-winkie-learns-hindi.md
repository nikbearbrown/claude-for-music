# Wee Willie Winkie Learns Hindi

SONG:

[Baritone Male Voice - Playful and Gentle]
Wee Willie Winkie runs through the shehar
Upar and neeche in pajamas there
Rapping at the khidki, calling through the lock
Are the bachche in bistar now, it is eight o'clock

[Baritone Male Voice - Learning Echo]
Shehar means town
Khidki means window
Bachche means children
Bistar means bed

[Baritone Male Voice - Whimsical and Fast]
He daudna past pillows and potted plants
He tripped on a giggle that learned to dance
He shook his fist at the sleepy aasmaan
Then winked at a baadal who winked back on

[Baritone Male Voice - Learning Echo]
Daudna means run
Aasmaan means sky
Baadal means cloud
Floating up high

[Baritone Male Voice - Mischievous Storytelling]
Down by the ped behind the big tree
A boy on a skateboard yelled not me
Up on the roof with mischief and glee
Two girls flew by on a kite made of tea

[Baritone Male Voice - Learning Echo]
Ped means tree
Khelna means play
Tez means fast
All through the day

[Baritone Male Voice - Bouncy Chorus]
Wee Willie Winkie runs through the town
Upar and neeche in his nightgown
Rapping at the khidki, crying through the lock
Are the bachche in bistar, nope, check the ghadi clock

[Baritone Male Voice - Silly and Bright]
Willie huffed and Willie puffed
Willie ran tez, but not enough
One jumped rope and one rode a gaay
One built spaghetti castles and shouted hooray

[Baritone Male Voice - Learning Echo]
Gaay means cow
Ghadi means clock
Sona means sleep
Tick tock, tick tock

[Baritone Male Voice - Moonlit and Playful]
Willie stomped his tired feet
No one is sleeping, they are all in the street
The chand is up and the taare are bright
Why won't you bachche say shubh ratri tonight

[Baritone Male Voice - Learning Echo]
Chand means moon
Taare means stars
Raat means night
Shubh ratri, goodnight

[Baritone Male Voice - Silly Animal Verse]
Out from shadows, what a sight
A snoring raccoon on a bicycle light
A hans in pajamas, a suar in a vest
A hamster in slippers said sleep, I protest

[Baritone Male Voice - Learning Echo]
Hans means goose
Suar means pig
Suraj means sun
Warm and big

[Baritone Male Voice - Sleepy and Funny]
Wee Willie Winkie threw up his hands
These bachche and janwar do not follow commands
Fine, stay awake, but I need my rest
Shubh ratri to all, I have tried my best

[Baritone Male Voice - Gentle Ending]
So Wee Willie trudged to his bistar bed
And snored so loud he shook his head
But the bachche kept khelna all night long
Till the suraj woke up and said you are wrong

[Baritone Male Voice - Final Chorus]
Wee Willie Winkie runs through the shehar
Upar and neeche in pajamas there
Khidki, bachche, bistar, ghadi
Learning Hindi happily

[Baritone Male Voice - Gentle Outro]
Sona means sleep
Khelna means play
Chand means moon
Suraj means day

Shubh ratri, goodnight
Taare shining bright
Wee Willie sleeps
Till morning light
