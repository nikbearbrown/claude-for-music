# The media's the most powerful entity on earth.

SONG:


[Spoken Word]

"The media's the most powerful entity on earth.
They have the power to make the innocent guilty
and to make the guilty innocent."

[Spoken Word, Whispered Delivery over ambient chimes]

"They sell you fear, division, and rage — then call it the truth."

[Verse 1 – Smooth Baritone]
In the shadowy corners, where cobwebs weave
Lives the Boogeyman — or so they believe
Not under beds or behind the door
He’s in every scroll, and wanting more

No face to see, he bends and flows
A shapeless whisper that everyone knows
Each home he takes a different hue
Crafted by the fear the night runs through

[Chorus – Whispered/Ethereal Harmony]
Boogeyman, Boogeyman, hidden so grand
No longer teaching with gentle hand
Not “eat your veggies,” not “say your prayers”
But “stay afraid, keep fighting theirs”

[Verse 2 – Low, Breathy Voice with Lo-fi Beat]
Some nights he’s loud, some days he’s meek
Some say he's gone — he just doesn’t speak
But one thing’s sure — his quiet plan
To feed you doubt, like only fear can

He’s not too far, just a screen away
Reminding you what not to say
Not “clean your room,” not “do good deeds”
But “pick a side, ignore their needs”

[Chorus – Deep Tenor with Ambient Screams Layered]
Boogeyman, Boogeyman, hidden so grand
Stirring up hate in every land
He doesn’t knock or haunt your dreams
He lives in loops and viral memes

[Bridge – Spoken Word + Fading Melody]
No bedtime story, no gentle fright
Just rage in pixels dressed as right
No longer teaching wrong from right
He teaches who to fear on sight

[Final Chorus – Whispered Falsetto Echoes]
Boogeyman, Boogeyman, a figure unseen
He teaches lessons both cruel and mean
Hidden (yes), in every land
In darkness now, he makes his stand

[Outro – Echoed Repeat, Lo-fi Whistle + Minor Chord Bells]

"They sell you fear, division, and rage — then call it the truth."
