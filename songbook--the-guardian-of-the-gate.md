# The Guardian of the Gate

SONG:

The Guardian of the Gate


lying still, breathe (poppies sway
and oh!) the lion’s heart almost-
sealed by a sigh of red blooms—
small paws & soft tails tugging, pull through
and then some (how curious
it is,
that mice so tiny—
could lift the mighty fall)

Lyin' still, breathin' poppy fields,
Lion’s roarin' heart almost sealed.
It took a field of mice to pull him through,
Tiny paws saved where the wild winds blew.

It's the smallest ones that help us stand,
On a path where courage holds our hand.
We're walkin' green roads, through gates of fate,
Toward the land of Oz, where wishes wait.

tiny things can carry (us)
through wide green fields
toward some dream of Oz,
where (courageously)
flowers bloom like wishes waiting

He thought he was mighty, big and bold,
But flowers brought him down, cold as cold.
Mice and friends picked him back up,
Heading for the yellow road, no more hiccups.

the road unfolds—green upon green
(doorways framed in whispering green)
and lion, so grand, sees these houses & fences,
painted the same emerald
as dreams,
and people with shy eyes, green as
the dawn
