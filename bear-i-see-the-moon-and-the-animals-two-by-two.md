# I See the Moon and the Animals Two by Two

SONG:

I see the moon and the moon sees me
The moon sees someone I long to see
God bless the moon and God bless me
And God bless the someone I long to see

Lord, keep us safe this night
Secure us from our fear
Let angels guard us while we sleep
Till morning light is near

The animals went in two by two, hurrah, hurrah
The animals went in two by two, hurrah, hurrah
The elephant and kangaroo
And two proud bears came lumbering through
And they all went into the ark
To get out of the rain

Two little dogs with tails that wag
Two grasshoppers in a paper bag
Two bluebirds in a nest of thread
Two sleepy owls with tilted heads

Two deer stepped softly through the dew
Two foxes blinked as the cold wind blew
Two snails crept slow on a leafy lane
Two cats curled up out of the rain

The animals came in two by two, hurrah, hurrah
They came in dancing, not in a queue, hurrah, hurrah
Two fireflies lit up the night
Two frogs jumped left and then to right
And they all went into the ark
To get out of the rain

God loves me, this I know
For whispers in the wind do show
That little ones to Him belong
They may be small, but He is strong
