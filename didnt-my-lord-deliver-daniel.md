# Didn’t my Lord deliver Daniel?

SONG:

[Spoken Word – Slow over gospel organ and tambourine roll]

Didn’t my Lord deliver Daniel?
Deliver Daniel, deliver Daniel?
Didn’t my Lord deliver Daniel?
And why not every man?

[Verse 1 – Spoken over soft acoustic guitar and foot-stomps]

Daniel served in Persia’s court
Under King Darius he held high report
But jealous men conspired with stealth
They tricked the king to pass a law for wealth

[Verse 2]

They said: “For thirty days, no one may pray
To any god or man but you, they must obey”
But Daniel, faithful, knelt three times each day
As was his custom — he refused to stray

[Bridge – Choir hum, organ swells]

They threw him in a den with lions fierce
But God shut their mouths — not a wound to pierce
That night he stayed, in darkness so deep
Yet emerged with joy — alive from the sleep

[Refrain – Call and Response w/ Choir]

Didn’t my Lord deliver Daniel?
Deliver Daniel, deliver Daniel?
Didn’t my Lord deliver Daniel?
And why not every man?

[Verse 3 – Spoken Word over slide guitar + organ swells]
