# I packed a sandwich late last night

SONG:

I packed a sandwich late last night
With pickles, cheese, and jam so bright
I set it on the window sill
And watched the stars go climb the hill

But when I turned my head away
The moon came out to laugh and play
It opened wide its cratered grin
And gobbled up my lunch again!

I said, Dear Moon, that wasn't right
You stole my dinner in the night!
