 Little Bo-Peep
Little Bo-Peep has lost her sheep,
And doesn’t know where to find them;
Leave them alone, and they’ll come home,
Wagging their tails behind them.

(Some versions continue for several more verses, but this is the most familiar stanza.)


Little Bo-Peep’s Wild Sheep Chase

[Verse]
Little Bo-Peep has lost her sheep,
They left without a sound,
She searched the field, she searched the barn,
But they were nowhere to be found.

[Verse]
She checked the woods, she checked the stream,
She peeked behind a tree,
And found a note upon a rock—
"We’ve gone to Tennessee!"

[Verse]
Bo-Peep sat down and cried a bit,
Then stomped her tiny feet,
"For sheep to leave without a word—
Well, that's just plain deceit!"

[Verse]
She packed a bag, she caught a train,
She headed to the south,
She asked a pig in overalls,
Who simply chewed his mouth.

[Verse]
A cow in boots said, "Oh dear me,
Your sheep? They’re gone for sure—
Last I saw, they hitched a ride
To dance in Baltimore!"

[Verse]
Bo-Peep chased trails both far and wide,
Through fields and farms and plains,
She found them at a county fair,
All riding on a train!

[Verse]
She grabbed their ears, she shook her head,
"You’ve given me such strife!"
But then she laughed and bought some pie,
Because, hey—that’s just sheep life.

[Chorus]
Sheep will roam, they love to play,
They never check the time—
Just let them be, and soon you’ll see,
They’ll come back in their prime!
