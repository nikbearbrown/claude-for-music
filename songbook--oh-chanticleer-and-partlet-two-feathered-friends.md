# Oh, Chanticleer and Partlet, two feathered friends,

SONG:

Oh, Chanticleer and Partlet, two feathered friends,
Through mountain and valley, their story wends.
A nut, a choke, and a tearful goodbye,
In life and in sorrow, they stayed side by side.

One fine day, they journeyed afar,
To mountains high, where the ripe nuts are.
“Let’s split the haul,” they both agreed,
But Partlet found one too big to eat.

She tried and tried, but the nut was stuck,
A choking fear—no breath, no luck.
“Get me water, or I’m done!” she cried,
And Chanticleer ran with all of his might.

To the river, he begged for some water sweet,
“To save my friend, who’s facing defeat!”
But the river replied, “Bring me a cord,”
And so to the bride, he ran in accord.
