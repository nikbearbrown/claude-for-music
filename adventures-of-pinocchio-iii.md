# Adventures of Pinocchio III

SONG:

Adventures of Pinocchio III

[Verse 1]
In Geppetto’s tiny, dusty room,
Where painted flames danced from the gloom,
A wooden child sprang to life too soon,
And the craftsman’s heart began to swoon.

[Verse 2]
With saw and chisel, Geppetto spun
A head, two arms, and hands of one
That, laughing loud, mocked father’s fun—
A prankster born, his deeds begun.

[Verse 3]
“Pinocchio, you’ll be my pride,”
Geppetto murmured, eyes open wide,
But the nose grew long, like waves in tide,
A puppet’s mischief could not hide.
