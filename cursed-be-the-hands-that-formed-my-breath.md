# Cursed be the hands that formed my breath,

SONG:

[Verse]
Cursed be the hands that formed my breath,
A gift of life, yet bound to death.
Why was I wrought in loathsome guise,
To wake beneath these scornful skies?

[Verse]
In midnight’s grasp, my anguish howled,
A beast unchained, a soul befouled.
