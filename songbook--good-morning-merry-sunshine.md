# (good morning,) merry sunshine,

SONG:

did you slip in (soft) and soon?
you (frightened) all the stars away,
and dimmed the old, (worn) moon.

did you slip in soft and soon?
you frightened all the stars away,

(how) did you wake so soon?

and shined away the moon.
