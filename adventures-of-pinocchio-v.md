# Adventures of Pinocchio V

SONG:

Adventures of Pinocchio V

[Verse 1]
Pinocchio’s belly rumbled loud,
As night fell soft, a darkening shroud.
With every minute, hunger grew,
And his mind spun with what to do.

[Verse 2]
Around he searched in frantic haste,
In every drawer, each nook and space,
But all he found, his nose confessed,
Was painted warmth, a cold jest.
