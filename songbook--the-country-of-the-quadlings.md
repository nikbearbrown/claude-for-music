# The Country of the Quadlings

SONG:

The Country of the Quadlings


They crossed through the forest, where shadows lay,
A steep hill ahead, with rocks in their way.
Scarecrow said, "We'll make it through, no doubt,"
But then came a voice that screamed, "Keep out!"

These hills belong to us, they cried,
With heads that shoot, they won’t be denied.
But we won’t fall, we’ll fly instead,
Over Hammer-Heads, through skies of red.
