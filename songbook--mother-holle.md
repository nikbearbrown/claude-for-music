# Mother Holle

SONG:

Mother Holle

Once there was a gentle girl,
worked each day without a whirl.
Sent to spin by the well so deep,
where her spindle took a leap.

She jumped in after, down below,
to a land where wonders grow.
Bread and apples called her near,
“Help us, please!” was all she'd hear.

She helped the bread, she shook the tree,
kind to each as kind could be.
Mother Holle took her in,
rewarded kindness, gold to skin.

But her sister, sly and sly,
ignored each plea, passed them by.
Holle sent her home in pitch,
selfish ways her only hitch.

So one came back with gold so bright,
the other stuck in pitch’s night.
Mother Holle gave each her due—
gold for the kind, and pitch for the shrew.
