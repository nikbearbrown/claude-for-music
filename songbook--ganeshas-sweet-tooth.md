# Ganesha's Sweet Tooth

Lyrics

In a temple tucked under a banyan tree,
Lives a jolly ol’ god who’s as sweet as can be.
With a trunk so long and ears spread wide,
It’s Ganesha, with a sweet tooth he can’t hide.

He munches on modaks, each a tasty treat,
Stuffed with jaggery and coconut—oh, so sweet!
Laddus round as the moon in a monsoon June,
He hums a little tune under the silver spoon.

“Oh dear, oh dear,” says he with a grin,
“I can’t stop eating, where do I begin?”
His belly’s quite round, his joy quite infectious,
For sweets make him boundlessly zealous.

From village to village, they know his taste,
Prepare the sweets, no time to waste.
For when Ganesha comes to bless the feast,
He looks for sweets, to say the least.

So if you hear a giggle by the sweets plate,
It’s just Ganesha, don’t make him wait.
Offer him a sweet, make it two, make it three,
And in return, good fortune there’ll be!

Main Characters

Ganesha: The beloved Hindu god with a sweet tooth, known for his love of modaks and laddus.
Villagers: Those who prepare and offer sweets to gain Ganesha’s blessings.
