# Adventures of Pinocchio IV

SONG:

Adventures of Pinocchio IV

[Verse 1]
Over meadows swift as breeze,
Pinocchio leapt through fields with ease,
Escaped from trouble, wild and free,
A wooden child, not yet to see.

[Verse 2]
Home he ran, his heart alight,
A fleeting joy, a brief respite.
But then a voice, so small and slight,
Chirped wisdom in the quiet night.
