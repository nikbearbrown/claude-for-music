# Down On Your Knees To Pray

SONG:

Down On Your Knees To Pray

Vocalization, Arrangement, and Production: Nik Bear Brown

As I went down on my knees to pray,
Hoping to find the good old way,
When you shall wear the starry crown,
Good Lord, show me the way.

O mourner, let's go down,
Down on my knees to pray.

As I went down on my knees to pray,
Hoping to find the good old way;
You shall wear the starry crown,
Good Lord, show me the way.

By-and-by I'll go down,
Down on my knees to pray.

Lift up your heads, Immanuel's friends,
And taste the pleasure Lord God sends.
Let nothing cause you to delay,
But hasten on the good old way.

As I went down on my knees to pray,
Seeking strength for the trials of the day,
With faith to guide me along the way,
Good Lord, show me the way.

O seeker, let me go forth,
Down on my knees to pray.
