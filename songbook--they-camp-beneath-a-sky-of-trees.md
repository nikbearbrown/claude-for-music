# they camp (beneath) a sky of trees

SONG:

they camp (beneath) a sky of trees
where stars drift close & shadows breathe
a fire crackles, (bread is broken)
they dream of Oz, their wishes spoken
They camped under stars, where shadows lay,
Seeking warmth by the fire at the end of the day.
With hope for the road, and fears close by,
They’re heading to Oz, beneath the sky.
the tin man’s axe (swings through night)
& scarecrow, clumsy with his hands,
gathers nuts in quiet grace
while the lion, hungry, finds his place
Underneath the trees, where the forest hides,
Tin Man chops wood, and the fire provides.
Toto and Dorothy eat the last of their bread,
Lion’s offer’s declined, no blood will be shed.
Scarecrow finds nuts for the morning to bring,
While the fire warms them like the kiss of spring.
come morning, they meet a ditch so wide
a leap (the lion—breath held tight)
each one crosses, one by one
a bound, a hope, a race toward sun
They camped under stars, where shadows lay,
Seeking warmth by the fire at the end of the day.
With hope for the road, and fears close by,
They’re heading to Oz, beneath the sky.
deep woods (again) & claws appear
kalidahs—wild—& the lion’s fear
they cut the tree, so it will fall
to carry them (the beasts and all)
At dawn they find a deep ditch ahead,
Jagged rocks below fill their minds with dread.
Lion says, “I’ll jump, with you on my back.”
One by one, across the gap, without a crack.
Each leap a breath held tight and fast,
They cross the void, their fears surpassed.
then water waits (so they build a raft)
small trees fall, the river laughs
as wood floats down to emerald dreams
where green fields rest, beneath the beams
They camped under stars, where shadows lay,
Seeking warmth by the fire at the end of the day.
With hope for the road, and fears close by,
They’re heading to Oz, beneath the sky
Through thick woods, the forest dark and deep,
