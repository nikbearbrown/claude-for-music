# The Fisherman and His Wife

SONG:

The Fisherman and His Wife

There was a fisherman by the sea,
Who lived in a pigsty, poor as could be.
He caught a fish, and it spoke with a plea,
“Let me go, I’m an enchanted royalty.”

Oh, a wish, a wish, what would you ask?
A fish with power for any task.
One wish granted, and then one more,
But greedy hearts keep asking for more.
