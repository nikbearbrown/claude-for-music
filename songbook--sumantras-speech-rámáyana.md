The childless king sat cloaked in thought,
For all he had, a son he sought.
A name to bear his legacy,
A branch to grow the royal tree.
He dreamed of blessings to receive,
A votive steed his heart’s reprieve.

Sumantra, wise with stories old,
A tale of prophecy retold:
Of sages from the dawn of time,
And words foretold in sacred rhyme.
"From Kasyap’s line," he said, "shall rise
A hermit boy, most pure and wise.

In forest shadows deep and still,
He lives apart, by father’s will.
