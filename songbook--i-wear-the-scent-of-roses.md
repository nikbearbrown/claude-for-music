# i wear the scent (of roses)

SONG:

i wear the scent (of roses)
wilting in this night’s
quiet sigh, i
offer my heart (beating)
but it slips, unheld,
through flight’s fingers.
I wear the scent of roses, wilted in the night,
Offer up my heartbeat, but it fades in flight.
Though my soul still trembles, with a desire you’ll never share,
I keep singing into silence, filling emptiness with air.
my soul—still trembling,
held by your not-love,
& in the empty echoing
i am singing / breathing / filling
spaces where you aren’t.
And I fear you’ve forgotten me, left me in the past,
I’m just a shadow in the rain, a thought that couldn’t last.
I wear the scent of roses, that wither in your light,
Still I stand here waiting, just a ghost in the night.
(for) got? (ten)
i haunt like shadows in the rain,
a thing that couldn’t— quite— stay
withering roses in your light’s ghost
i wait / and fade, fade as
(a scent in the night).
Your smile, a distant memory, a dream that fades away,
I trace the sky with fingers, wishing clouds could stay.
I stand beneath your window, but you look past my face,
Warmth slipping through fingers, lost in empty space.
& stars, like lovers far
away, still blink;
petals falling
(silent, distant),
yet there’s no (you)
here to catch
their fainting whispers.
But you don’t see me, you don’t hear my cry,
I’m lost in the shadows, where love goes to die.
Oh, you don’t feel me, you don’t know my pain,
I’m just a flower in the rain, calling your name in vain.
your eyes (yes)
deeper than all roses
and louder than my heart
i am quiet, & the night slips (by)
carrying the words
i might have (said)
drifting past your (closed) window.
