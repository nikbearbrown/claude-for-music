# Hansel and Gretel, lost and alone,

SONG:

Hansel and Gretel, lost and alone,
In a forest dark, where shadows are grown.
With courage and wit, and love to keep near,
They find their way back, no more to fear.

Once there was hunger, their father in strife,
A poor woodcutter, a struggling life.
With stepmother’s whisper, plans took their shape,
“Lead them to the woods; there’s no escape.”

Hansel, so clever, gathered bright stones,
Glittering pebbles, marking their way home.
While stepmother schemed, and the forest grew,
Hansel knew how to see the journey through.

Hansel and Gretel, lost and alone,
In a forest dark, where shadows are grown.
With courage and wit, and love to keep near,
They find their way back, no more to fear.

But once again, food ran low,
And deep in the woods, they were left to go.
With breadcrumbs crumbled to mark their way,
But birds came by and stole them away.

Lost in the forest, starving and weak,
They stumbled upon a house unique—
Made of sugar, cakes, and sweets,
A witch’s trap in the forest deep.

Hansel and Gretel, lost and alone,
In a forest dark, where shadows are grown.
With courage and wit, and love to keep near,
They find their way back, no more to fear.
