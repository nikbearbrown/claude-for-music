# The Traveling Musicians - Shel Silverstein Style

SONG:

The Traveling Musicians - Shel Silverstein Style

The donkey was old, his bones felt the weight,
The farmer said, "You’re done, old mate."
But the donkey had dreams, big as a moon,
"Why, I’ll be a musician; I’ll bray me a tune!"

Down the road he met an old dog with a sigh,
"I’m tired," said the dog, "but I’m too young to die."
"Join me!" said the donkey, "we’ll sing on the way,
A dog’s bark in a band? We’ll make it okay."

A little further on, they spotted a cat,
Looking grumpy and worn, squashed down flat.
"My mistress tried to drown me for being slow!"
"Join us!" said donkey, "to the city we’ll go!"

Then came a rooster, crowing with might,
“Tomorrow,” he cried, “I’ll be dinner tonight!”
