# Near to the Heart of God


**Date/source note:** 1903

## Background

1903 He will gather the lambs with his arm and carry them in his bosom. Isaiah 40 verse
11 Park University in Parkville, Missouri, with 38 campuses across the United States,
boasts of an enrollment of over 17,000 students. It was begun in 1875 with only 17
students by John A. McAfee and Colonel George Park, the colorful founder of Parkville
who donated the land. McAfee had five sons and a daughter who all became involved in the
college. The fourth son, Cleveland, graduated from what was then Park College after
studying at Union Theological Seminary.

He returned to Park as Chaplain and Choir Director. Cleveland's daughter, Catherine,
later told how her father came to write the great hymn
