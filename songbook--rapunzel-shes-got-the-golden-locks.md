# Rapunzel, she's got the golden locks

SONG:

Rapunzel, she's got the golden locks
Impossibly long, like tick-tock, TikTok
Climbin' up the tower, she's got the power
Flow so fresh, let it shower, hour after hour

Hair so long, it ain't even right
She be spinnin' rhymes from dusk 'til daylight
Long like a whip, she's got that hair game
Rapunzel's got the swag, she's in her own lane

Rapunzel, let your hair down, let it flow
No need for a ladder, you're in control
You're the queen of the tower, you're the crown holder
Rapunzel, let your voice be heard, never fold ya

Rapunzel, Rapunzel, high in the tower,
Locked away, waiting hour by hour.
Love and hope, through joy and strife,
Together they'll find their way in life.

(let down)

all the weight of time

hair long as a whisper of night,
a whip through shadows
and light—she spins words
dusk 'til daylight,
lines unwind like silver threads

Rapunzel, she lets it flow,
no ladder, no climb, just—go
she, the queen who holds
her crown
above and far below

and so (high in the sky) she sings,
hour by hour—(her voice)
falls like rain, through joy, through pain,
they’re bound to find
the way

Rapunzel—golden threads of dreams
woven through love and strife
all tangled in the waiting time,
