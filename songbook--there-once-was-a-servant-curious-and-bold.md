# There once was a servant, curious and bold,

SONG:

"The White Snake":

[Verse]
There once was a servant, curious and bold,
Who tasted a dish the king kept controlled.
A bite of white snake, a nibble, a chew,
And soon he heard whispers from animals too.

[Verse]
A duck with a ring stuck deep in her belly,
Three gasping fish trapped in muck and in jelly,
An ant with a grievance, a raven in need—
Our servant helped all, both in thought and deed.

[Chorus]
Oh, one good turn deserves one more,
A gift comes knocking at your door.
Help a friend, you’ll see the day
