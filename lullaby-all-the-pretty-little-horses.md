# Lullaby: All the Pretty Little Horses

SONG:

All the Pretty Little Horses
Hush-a-bye, don't you cry,
Go to sleepy little baby.
When you wake you shall have,
All the pretty little horses.

Blacks and bays, dapples and grays,
Coach and six white horses.
Hush-a-bye, don't you cry,
Go to sleepy little baby.
