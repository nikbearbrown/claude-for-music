# The Mark of Death

SONG:

The Mark of Death
A Poem in the Style of Mary Wollstonecraft Shelley

[Verse 1]
The lantern’s glow on sand-strewn shore,
Revealed a corpse, so cold and pale.
His lips, once bright, would breathe no more,
His throat bore death’s unyielding tale.
Oh, cruel night! Oh, hand accursed!
Did fate now claim my dearest friend?
