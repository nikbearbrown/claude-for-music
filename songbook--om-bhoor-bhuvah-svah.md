# Om Bhoor-Bhuvah Svah

SONG:

Om Bhoor-Bhuvah Svah
Song lyrics by Nik Bear Brown https://soundcloud.com/nik-bear-brown

In the realm of pure spirituality,
Shines a soul with divine clarity.
Gayatri,oh beauty and grace,
Your radiance fills every space.

Om Bhoor-Bhuvah Svah,
Tat Savitur Varenyam,
Bhargo Devasya Dheemahi,
Dhiyo Yo Nah Prachodayat.

Om,eternal and profound,
Bringing solace,where peace is found.
Tat Savitur,the supreme choice,
Guiding hearts with a silent voice.

Gayatri,love and light,
Om Bhoor-Bhuvah Svah takes flight.
Bhargo Devasya,our minds align,
Dhiyo Yo Nah Prachodayat—spirits shine.

In silence or sound,Om resonates,
A cosmic essence that creates.
From earth to sky,realms unfold,
In awe,we stand,stories untold.

Bhargo Devasya,in His light,
Our hearts align,shining bright.
His wisdom fills the darkest night,
Guiding us on paths of right.

In Gayatri’s light,we bow,
With every chant,a sacred vow.
Let our spirits soar and shine,
In her embrace,we find the divine.

Om Bhoor-Bhuvah Svah,
Tat Savitur Varenyam,
Bhargo Devasya Dheemahi,
Dhiyo Yo Nah Prachodayat.
