# The Dog and the Sparrow

SONG:

The Dog and the Sparrow

There once was a dog, sad and thin,
His master was cruel, no food for him.
The dog ran off with sorrow and shame,
Met a sparrow who asked, “What’s your name?”

Oh, the dog and the sparrow, a strange little pair,
One who could fly, and one with despair.
Together they traveled, together they’d roam,
Till they found the dog a friend and a home.
