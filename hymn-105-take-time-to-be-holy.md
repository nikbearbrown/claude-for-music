# Take Time to Be Holy


**Date/source note:** 1882

## Background

1882. Be holy for I am holy. 1 Peter 1 verse 16 The words to take time to be holy were
written about 1882 by William Longstaff, a wealthy Englishman who served as treasurer of
the Bethesda, free chapel in Sunderland, a port city in northeast England. His church
hosted the first meetings held by D.L. Moody and Iris Sanky in that area, and Longstaff
became a great supporter of the two. In his book of hymns stories, Iris Sanky said that
take time to be holy was prompted by a sermon William heard in New Brighton on the text,
Be holy for I am holy.
