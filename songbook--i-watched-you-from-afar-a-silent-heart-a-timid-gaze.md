# i watched you (from afar) a silent heart (a timid gaze)

SONG:
i watched you (from afar) a silent heart (a timid gaze)
you, the light (unknown) i needed through my days
with courage bound (by quiet) i stood in shadows deep
now left (with prayers) and starry nights where silence sleeps

i watched you (from afar) a silent heart (a timid gaze)
never knew that grace (like yours) could walk in simple stride
embodied grace (her charm) in rivers flowing wide
you stood in golden sunlight, warmth i cannot claim
and i, a whispered silence, left calling your name

you walked past (and i stood still) your smile, a world to see
god gave me eyes to look at you (but not the words to speak)
your beauty haunts my quiet nights like rain upon the trees
and i am hoping still, not too late (that you might notice me)

i watched you (from afar) a silent heart (a timid gaze)
you, the queen (a friend) in my shadows cast
her spirit lifts the soul, light (for every dawn)
each step brings peace, burdens fall, hope unfolds
now left (with prayers) and starry nights where silence sleeps

in every raindrop (i see your face) tender in its glow
like moonlight on a sea (the warmth i’ll never know)
you’ve always been there (in my sight) but hidden from my view
my world so cold and empty now, like boston skies, so blue

i watched you (from afar) a silent heart (a timid gaze)
each glance (a melody) each laugh a song so bright
your eyes a world unknown (a song beyond my sight)
you walked the quiet spaces, a heartbeat in the air
a voice of all things distant (beautiful and rare)

i watched you (from afar) a silent heart (a timid gaze)
a queen, a friend (blessings never cease)
left without goodbye, a heart bound up in fear
between the words i should have said (and what you couldn’t hear)
now shadows hold the wishes (too weak to let me say)
caught between the sun and you (and hoping you might stay)

i watched you (from afar) a silent heart (a timid gaze)
for you are like the morning sun (that warms and bends and mends)
a soul that fills the air with light (no sorrow can defend)
so here i stand in silent hope (beneath a sky so wide)
that you will see the love i hold, with blessings by my side

i watched you (from afar) a silent heart (a timid gaze)
