# There was a rich man and his wife so fair,

SONG:

"The Juniper Tree":

[Verse]
There was a rich man and his wife so fair,
But one thing they wanted, and it wasn’t there.
She prayed every day for a child, you see,
And stood peeling apples by the juniper tree.

[Verse]
Her wish came true, but so did sorrow,
A baby boy born as red as tomorrow,
With skin as white as winter’s snow—
But joy was brief; she had to go.
