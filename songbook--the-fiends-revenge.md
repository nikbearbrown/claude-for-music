# The Fiend’s Revenge

SONG:


The Fiend’s Revenge
A Poem in the Style of Mary Wollstonecraft Shelley

[Verse 1]
The tempest howled, the heavens wept,
As darkness claimed the bridal room.
No star above in mercy kept,
No hand of fate to stay the doom.
I held my breath, my fingers tight
Upon the pistol at my chest—
A shadow stirred against the night,
A whisper stole my fleeting rest.
