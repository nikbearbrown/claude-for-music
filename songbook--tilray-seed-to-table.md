Vertical Integration Challenges

Seed to sale is expensive but necessary due to regulatory requirements.


Regulatory compliance costs vary dramatically across jurisdictions

Capital requirements for maintaining GMP (Good Manufacturing Practice) standards vary

Varying climate conditions affecting cultivation strategy (Canada's cold climate is suboptimal)

Strategic Adaptation Opportunities

To strengthen their position while awaiting broader liberalization.

Supply Chain Optimization: Blockchain implementation could improve certification and transparency while potentially allowing specialization at different stages

Market Diversification: Expansion into wellness drinks, cuisine, and lifestyle products makes sense as a bridge strategy until cannabis-specific regulations evolve

Regulatory Expertise: Positioning as regulatory experts who can help shape evolving laws gives them competitive advantage in new markets

Geographic Flexibility: Maintaining cultivation capacity in optimal growing regions while establishing processing capabilities in key consumer markets
