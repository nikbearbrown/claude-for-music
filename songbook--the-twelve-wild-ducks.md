# The Twelve Wild Ducks

SONG:

The Twelve Wild Ducks

Once on a time there was a Queen out driving in the winter snow
She bled at the nose and saw red blood on the white snow
She thought of her twelve sons and wished for a daughter instead
A troll witch came and promised her one
But the sons would belong to her after the christening

Soon a daughter was born as white as snow and as red as blood
They called her Snow-white and Rosy-red
But when she was christened the twelve princes turned into wild ducks
They flew away and were never seen again
