# Oh, Muffin

SONG:

Oh, Muffin

[Verse]
It sits on the plate, so tempting and fine,
A muffin so perfect, almost divine.
I reach out to grab it, feel the warmth in my hand,
But rules are rules, one a day's the plan.

[Chorus]
Oh, muffin, my treat, you're a sight to behold,
Golden and sweet, or chocolatey bold.
Just one per morning, by the kitchen light,
Savoring slowly, making it just right.

[Verse]
