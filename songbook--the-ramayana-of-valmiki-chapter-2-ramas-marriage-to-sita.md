# The Ramayana of Valmiki – Chapter 2: Rama’s Marriage to Sita

Styles: gospel folk, acoustic country, banjo, raspy vocals, violin, tabla, whistling, soulful, humming

[Spoken Word Intro – Male Voice, poetic and reverent with soft harmonium drone]

Let’s learn some Hindi vocabulary from a wedding divine
Where love bloomed with dharma and fate did align
No textbook can teach how a bow can bind

We sing not of rules, but of choices and grace
Of strength shown in silence, in Sita’s face
From “vivah” to “dhanush”, from “swayamvar” to “prarthna”
This is how we learn—not by lesson, but by mantra
