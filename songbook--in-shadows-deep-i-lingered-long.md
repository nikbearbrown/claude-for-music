# In shadows deep, I lingered long,

SONG:

[Verse]
In shadows deep, I lingered long,
A specter bound by fate’s cruel song.
Through hovel cracks, I watched unseen,
A world so bright, yet cold and mean.

[Verse]
Their voices soft, their hearts so pure,
Each act of love, a balm, a cure.
Yet in my breast, a tempest swirled,
For I was cast beyond their world.

[Verse]
