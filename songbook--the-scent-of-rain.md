# the scent of rain

SONG:

the scent of rain

the scent of rain
when the clouds gather
(and skies dim low)
the rain begins its pitter-patter
softly, slow—

the scent of rain
when the clouds gather and skies start to dim,
and the pitter-patter of rain begins,
there’s a scent in the air, fresh and profound,
that sweeps through the streets and over the ground.

the scent of rain
a scent, fresh, profound—
sweeps through streets (over
the ground)

the scent of rain
it's not just water
falling
from the sky
but secrets mixing (flying high)—
plant oils,
bacteria,
ozone too—
make the sweet smell
so fresh
so new

the scent of rain
from deep in the soil, the bacteria rise,
with the rain they awake, and reach for the skies.
the plants release oils, a gift to the air,
while ozone lends a crispness that’s beyond compare.

the scent of rain
from soil's deep veins
bacteria rise
with rain they awaken,
reach for skies—
the plants release oils
(a gift to the air)
while ozone crisps (beyond compare)

the scent of rain
it's not just water
falling
from the sky
but whispers mingling (flying high)—
plant oils,
bacteria,
ozone too—
make the sweet smell
so fresh
so new

the scent of rain
so next time it rains, take a moment to stand,
and breathe in the magic that fills the land.
for every droplet carries a tale,
of earthy aromas that together sail.

the scent of rain
stand still when it rains—
breathe the magic in
as droplets carry tales
(earth’s sweet perfume
whirls through the wind)

the scent of rain
it's not just water
falling
from the sky
but nature’s dance, a lullaby—
plant oils,
bacteria,
ozone too—
make the sweet smell
so fresh
so new

the scent of rain
let this melody of nature never cease to amaze,
as it dances and mingles in the rainy day haze.
for in every scent, there’s a story to tell,
of the earth’s own perfume, in which we dwell.

the scent of rain
in every scent
a story dwells
of earth’s perfume
where we dwell.

the scent of rain
