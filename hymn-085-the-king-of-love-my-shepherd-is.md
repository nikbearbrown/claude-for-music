# The King of Love My Shepherd Is


**Date/source note:** 1868

## Background

1868. Ye, though I walk through the valley of the shadow of death, I will fear no evil,
for you are with me. You are rod and your staff, they comfort me. Psalm 23 verse 4 This
is among the most beautiful of all the renditions of the 23rd Psalm. It was written by
Henry Williams Baker, born in London on May 27, 1821, the oldest son of a vice admiral
in the British Navy. Henry attended Trinity College Cambridge and was ordained in 1844
at age 24. After serving as an assistant pastor for several years, he became the vicar
of Monkland, Herfarchir, in 1851.

That same year upon his father's death, he was knighted and became a baronette. For
years Henry worked on a book of hymns that would reflect the grandeur of majestic
worship. The first edition was published in 1861 entitled hymns, ancient, and modern. On
the British Isles, it was known by its initials, H-A-M. Because Henry had labored so
earnestly over every hymn, editing and changing and deleting words, some called it
hymns, asked for, and mutilated. But it became the leading hymn book in the Anglican
Church, going through many revisions and selling over 150 million copies.

The King of Love didn't appear until the 1868 revision in the appendix. When Henry
passed away in 1877, his friend John Ellerton reported that his last words were from
this great hymn. Perverse and foolish oft-i-stayed, but yet in love he sought me, and on
his shoulder gently laid, and home rejoicing brought me. Henry wrote many other lesser-
known hymns, one of the finest is his morning hymn to be recommended for all upon
rising. My father for another night of quiet sleep and rest, for all the joy of morning
light by holy name be blessed.

Now with a newborn day I give myself a new to thee, that as thou willest I may live, and
what thou willest be. What air I do things great or small, what air I speak or frame,
thy glory may I seek an all, do all in Jesus' name. My father for his sake, thy pray thy
child except and bless, and lead me by thy grace today in paths of righteousness
