# betty botter bought some bitter butter

SONG:

betty botter bought some bitter butter
(but oh she bit her) better—
and her tongue turned tart, a twisting trip
of bitter bites (a buttered slip)

big black bug bit big black bear
crunch pop thud (bitterer)
while bear’s big paw went thump-thud-thud,
bug skittered off, through dirt and mud

six (slippery sliding) snails
slid south slowly slowly so—
each one trailed a silvery string,
a shimmering, glimmering, slippery thing

fuzzy wuzzy (no hair bear)
scratched his head (quite bare, quite bare)
wondered where his fur had flown
and felt his skin, cold as stone

susie sitting shines her shop
she polishes bright and she doesn’t stop
each bottle, buckle, bell, and blade—
gleaming things, all neatly displayed

peter piper (picked his peck)
of pickled peppers, puckered neck—
but sour slipped, then dripped and dropped
‘till peter’s peck of pickles popped

tiny timmy tugged a thread
then spun it round his bobblehead—
"tick-tock-tick" his feet went down
in crazy spins all through the town

big brown toad (on tippy-toe)
wobbled out to steal the show—
he croaked, he hopped, with flair and gleam
(his toadish self the village dream)

green grasshopper skipped up high
with leaps and loops across the sky—
wings outstretched, a tiny kite
zigzagged right, then left to light

happy hippo hiccuped, hopped
down the road, he never stopped
with hiccups big and hops galore,
on through fields forevermore

happy hippo hiccups hops,
through hollow hums
and empty stops,

betty botter’s butter bitter,
and big black bugs go crunch (critter by critter)

oh, fuzzy wuzzy no-hair bear,
how did you end up so bare?
while sally sells the seashore’s shells,
six snails slide south (slowly so)

peter piper’s popper peppers,
pickled pack upon his platter—
and woodchucks chuck, wood wood wood
(only if they could could could)

swiss wristwatches ticking ticking,
biscuits boxed and brisk blades flicking—
red yellow red yellow red,
the tricky tongue that’s never fed

hippo hiccups hops and hums,
through hollow homes and hoots, and hums
a happy hippo’s hiccuped song,
in a world where silly sounds belong
