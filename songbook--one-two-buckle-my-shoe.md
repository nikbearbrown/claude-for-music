 One, Two, Buckle My Shoe
One, two,
Buckle my shoe;
Three, four,
Knock at the door;
Five, six,
Pick up sticks;
Seven, eight,
Lay them straight;
Nine, ten,
A big fat hen;
Eleven, twelve,
Dig and delve;
Thirteen, fourteen,
Maids a-courting;
Fifteen, sixteen,
Maids in the kitchen;
Seventeen, eighteen,
Maids a-waiting;
Nineteen, twenty,
My plate’s empty.

One, Two, What Did I Do?
[Verse]
One, two,
Buckle my shoe.
Three, four,
Knock at the door—
But who’s outside? A talking bear!
With purple socks and spiky hair!

[Verse]
Five, six,
Pick up sticks.
Seven, eight,
Lay them straight.
But wait—why are they glowing blue?
And why’d they just turn into glue?

[Verse]
Nine, ten,
A big fat hen.
Eleven, twelve,
Dig and delve.
But when I dug, oh what a sight!
A dancing worm in pants too tight!

[Verse]
Thirteen, fourteen,
Maids a-courting.
Fifteen, sixteen,
Maids in the kitchen.
But what’s this nonsense? I declare!
A fish is baking pancakes there!

[Verse]
Seventeen, eighteen,
Maids a-waiting.
Nineteen, twenty,
My plate’s empty!
