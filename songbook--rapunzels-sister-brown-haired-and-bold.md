rapunzel's sister, brown-haired & bold
she stands high, against a towered sky
her hair like ladders to the clouds, unspooled
in twists and turns, she lets it fly

her strands braid sunlight, weaving scars away
they tumble down, like freedom's song
a Brown-eyed Queen, fierce & rare
free from chains, her spirit bare

ain't waiting for no prince
her story’s in her own two hands
free to climb, free to stand
hair flowing like a rebel’s dance

Imma tell a tale 'bout Rapunzel's sister, a beauty so fine
brown hair flowing down her back, redefining design
an Indian princess, beyond compare
with impossibly long hair, reaching sky, air

she be lettin' it down, letting worries fly
breakin' chains, taking back her identity
holdin' power, her own royalty
crafting a legacy of quiet grace

a thousand secrets in each strand
whispered on the forest breeze
her steps alone, no guide in sight
she moves with purpose, pure and bright

ain't waiting for no prince
her path unfolds in steps, in dreams
through tangled woods and sunlit beams
her crown is hers, her reign supreme

rapunzel's sister’s rebellion, she breaks through
defies walls that once confined
true to herself, she’ll rise above it all
with flowing locks and daring climb

her voice a thread, her spirit clear
in every tangle, free to grow
an anthem spun in each small breath
to rise, to climb, and still to go

she's free, a force, a rebel beat
hair like ladders to the stars
a journey written in her steps
her beauty fierce, her soul complete
