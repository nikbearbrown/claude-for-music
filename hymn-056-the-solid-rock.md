# The Solid Rock


**Date/source note:** 1834

## Background

1834 For no other foundation can anyone lay than that which is laid, which is Jesus
Christ. First Corinthians chapter 3 verse 11 Edward Mote was born in Depoverty on
January 21, 1797 in London. His parents, in keepers, wouldn't allow a Bible in their
house. But somehow Edward heard the gospel as a teenager and came to Christ. He
eventually became a skilled carpenter and the owner of his own cabinet shop. One morning
he recalled, it came into my mind as I went to labor to write a hymn on the gracious
experience of a Christian.

As I went up to Holborn, I had the chorus. On Christ
