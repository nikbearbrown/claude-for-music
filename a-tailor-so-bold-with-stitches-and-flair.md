# A tailor so bold, with stitches and flair,

SONG:

A tailor so bold, with stitches and flair,
Beat giants and unicorns, boars in his lair.
With "seven at once," he'd proudly declare,
That no one, not even a king, could compare.

There once was a tailor, so small yet sly,
Who slapped seven flies with a single try.
He stitched up a belt, letters so proud:
"Seven at one blow!" he said aloud.

Into the world with cheese and a bird,
He strutted so proudly, his bravery stirred.
Met a giant who squeezed stones till they bled,
But the tailor’s cheese trick had the giant misled.

Outsmarted two giants at rest by a tree,
He dropped stones on their heads till they fought viciously.
They tore up the earth, threw branches around,
Until both lay defeated, dead on the ground.
