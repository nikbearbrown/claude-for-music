CAT AND MOUSE IN PARTNERSHIP

[Shel Silverstein Style]
[Verse 1]
Once a cat so sly and sweet,
Met a mouse so small and neat,
"Let’s be friends and share a home,
And never let our bellies groan!"

[Verse 2]
"We’ll need some food," the cat did say,
"To keep us fed on winter’s day.
A pot of fat, let’s tuck away,
Beneath the church—safe to stay!"

[Verse 3]
But oh, the cat had a sneaky plan,
A liar, a trickster, a scheming scam!
Said she, “A christening calls me now,
A baby mouse? I must take a bow!”

[Verse 4]
She licked the pot, "Oh what a treat!"
And walked the rooftops, feeling sweet.
She came back home with a grin so wide,
"The baby’s name? 'Top-Off!'" she lied.

[Verse 5]
Again she left, again she stole,
Licking fat down to the bowl.
She came back home, her belly tight,
And said, "His name? 'Half-Done!' Alright?"
