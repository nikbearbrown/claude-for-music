# shepherds (kept their watch)

SONG:

shepherds (kept their watch)

while shepherds watched (their flocks by night)
all seated (on the ground)
an angel of the lord came down—
& glory (shone around)

"fear not" (said he) for dread & dark
had seized (their troubled mind)
"glad tidings (of great joy i bring)
to you & all mankind"

"in david’s town (this very day)
is born (of david’s line)
a savior (christ the lord, the light)
& this shall be the sign:"

"a child (so small, so heaven-sent)
wrapped (in swaddling tight)
laid (within a manger low)
beneath the starlit night"

thus spoke (the angel) suddenly—
a chorus (robed in fire)
angels filled the sky (in song)
with voices rising higher:

"glory (to god on high)
peace (upon the earth)
to those who rest (within his grace)
a love (of boundless worth)"

the shepherds rose (in silent awe)
to seek (the holy child)
through quiet fields (where starlight led)
their hearts (with hope beguiled)

they found him (in the humble stall)
just as (the angel said)
with mary near (and joseph kind)
they knelt (beside his bed)

with grateful hearts (they sang his name)
for heaven’s gift (to save)
then spread the news (of love & light)
to all—the gift he gave
