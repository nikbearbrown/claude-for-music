# In a land of forests and trees so tall,

SONG:
In a land of forests and trees so tall,
Lived Little Red-Cap,loved by all.
With her red velvet cap,she roamed each day,
Known for her kindness,she'd give it away.

One morning her mother,with loving care,
Said,"Take this cake and wine,Little Red-Cap fair.
To your grandma's house,now quickly go,
She's weak and ill,and these will make her glow."

Red-Cap set off,not a worry in sight,
But a wolf met her,eyes gleaming bright.
"Good day,Little Red-Cap," he said with a smile,
"Where are you off to,in such grand style?"

"To my grandma's," she replied,unafraid,
"With cake and wine,a visit's made."
But the wolf,so sly,had a plan in mind,
To catch both Red-Cap and grandma,unkind.
