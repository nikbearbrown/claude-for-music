# Another Year Is Dawning


**Date/source note:** 1874

## Background

1874. Within me a clean heart, O God, and renew a steadfast spirit within me. Psalm 51
verse 10. In many churches, the first Sunday of the year wouldn't be complete without
singing, Francis Ridley Havagall's great, another year is dawning. Its words reflect the
deep consecration that marked your poems. Francis considered every new year's day a
never-to-be-missed opportunity of rededication to Christ, and several New Year's hymns
came from her pen. This particular poem, another year is dawning, was written as a
prayer for the beginning of 1874.

Francis composed it near the end of the old year and had it preded on a specially
designed greeting card to be sent to friends. The caption said, a happy New Year ever
such may it be. As it turned out, Francis herself needed that prayer, because just a few
days later she suffered a stunning disappointment. She was hoping to be launched as an
author in America, and her agent in New York had made reassuring promises. Then came a
letter she thought would bear the first of many royalty checks. Instead it reported that
her publisher had gone bankrupt in the stock market crash of 1873.

But as Francis had only recently turned all her affairs over to the Lord, she bore the
crisis with peace, writing to a friend, I have just had such a blessing in the shape of
what would have been only two months ago a really bitter blow to me. I was expecting a
letter from America in closing thirty-five pounds now do me, and possibly news that my
book was going on like steam. The letter has come, and instead of all this, my publisher
has failed in the universal crash. He holds my written promise to publish only with him
as the condition of his launching me.

So this is not simply a little loss, but an end of all my American prospects. I really
had not expected that he would do for me so much above all I asked, as not merely to
help me to acquiesce in this, but positively not to feel it at all, and only to rejoice
in it as a clear test of the reality of victorious faith, which I do find brightening
all most daily. Two months ago this would have been a real trial to me, for I had built
a good deal on my American prospects. Now, I will be done. It's not a sigh, but only a
song

## Lyrics

Another year is dawning, dear Father, let it be, in working or in waiting another year
with thee. Another year of progress, another year of praise, another year of proving by
presence all the days. Another year of dawning
