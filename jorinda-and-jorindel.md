# JORINDA AND JORINDEL

---

## 1. Plain Lyrics (DistroKid)

(A Poem Inspired by Shel Silverstein
And E.E. Cummings)
Once in a wood so shadowed and deep
A castle stood where the lost birds sleep
A fairy dwelled, with a cruel, sharp gaze
Trapping love in her twilight maze
The young who wandered, she turned to stone
Or caged as birds in her halls alone
Jorinda sang by the birch’s sway
“well-a-day! well-a-day!”

Her song broke sharp, her voice undone
As nightingale notes replaced the sun
Jorindel froze, like roots in frost
Watching helpless, his love was lost
The owl flew thrice with a fiery cry
“tu-whu! tu-whu! the night will lie.”
The fairy came, with her chin and nose
A hook of darkness where sorrow grows
Jorinda caged, her song forlorn
Jorindel wept for the coming morn

His dreams brought hope, a flower so rare
Purple petals with a pearl to bear
It whispered soft of the fairy’s doom
Of love restored in the shadowed gloom
Eight days he searched, through hill and glen
On the ninth, his fate began again
With the flower’s touch, the castle bent
Doors flew wide where his steps were sent
Birds in cages, their songs all one
A thousand melodies under no sun

Jorindel paused, unsure and scared
Which of the voices was hers declared?
The fairy screamed, her rage alight
But the flower’s glow held back her might
One cage he touched, and love returned
As Jorinda’s eyes with fire burned
Arms entwined, they broke the spell
And freed the birds from their shadowed hell
Love walked free, where the woods once mourned
The castle empty, its power scorned

Jorinda and Jorindel made their way
“well-a-day!” they’d sing, “well-a-day!”
Their joy a song, their bond unbroken
A tale of love where dreams are spoken

## 4. Session Notes

**Key:** G major
**Tempo:** Slow ballad ~68 BPM
**Time Signature:** 4/4
**Feel:** Mournful, sparse, building to cathartic release
**Style:** folk musical, acoustic country, storytelling pop, hip-hop, trap, spoken word

---

**Form Map:**

| Section | Bars (approx.) | Notes |
|---------|---------------|-------|
| Intro | 4 | Soft vocal warm-up with acoustic guitar establishing tone |
| Verse 1 | 8 | Sparse lead vocal, story-forward phrasing |
| Chorus | 8 | Harmonies enter, wider dynamics |
| Bridge | 4 | Textural shift and emotional lift |
| Outro | 4 | Fade with ambient tail and final refrain energy |

---

**Instrument List:**
- Acoustic Guitar: primary rhythm bed and song movement
- Violin: melodic countermelodies and emotional color
- Harmonica: melodic countermelodies and emotional color
- Drum Machine: pulse, groove, and dynamic movement
- Bass: pulse, groove, and dynamic movement

---

**Section-by-Section Notes:**

**Intro:** Keep the warm-up intimate and centered, with the lead instrument setting the harmonic bed.
**Verse 1:** Keep accompaniment sparse so the text stays intelligible; favor close, conversational vocal delivery.
**Chorus:** Add harmonies, broaden the stereo image, and let the rhythm section support the emotional peak.
**Bridge:** Shift texture with a held chord, solo response, or reduced percussion before returning to the main feel.
**Outro:** Let the final phrase breathe, then fade with reverb or natural ambience.

---

**Production Notes:**
Build the track around lyric clarity first, then add color in small layers. Keep the mix spacious enough for the vocal to carry the story, with the strongest dynamic lift reserved for the chorus or final refrain. Use the inferred style blend as a palette rather than a rigid genre box.
