# Under His Wings


**Date/source note:** 1896

## Background

1896. He shall cover you with His feathers, and under His wings you shall take refuge.
Psalm 91, verse 4. In the days following World War II, when communists were overrunning
China, the endangered missionaries found comfort knowing that in a world dominated by
the iron curtain and the bamboo curtain, they could rest under the feather curtain of
God. Psalm 91, verse 4 says, He shall cover you with His feathers, and under His wings
you shall take refuge. The biblical patriarch, Boaz, referred to Ruth as a woman who had
come under the Lord's wings for refuge, Ruth chapter 2, verse 12.

Later Ruth's great grandson, King David, perhaps recalling that story, asked the Lord in
Psalm 17, verse 8, Keep me as the apple of your eye, hide me under the shadow of your
wings, and in Psalm 57 he cried, Be merciful to me, O God, be merciful to me, for my
soul trusts in you, and in the shadow of your wings I will make my refuge until these
calamities have passed by. A thousand years later David's great descendant, Jesus of
Nazareth, said to the people of Jerusalem, O Jerusalem, Jerusalem, the one who kills the
prophets and stones those who were sent to her, how often I wanted to gather your
children together as a hen gathers her chicks under her wings.

All these comforting word pictures were woven together in the hymn
