# sometimes it’s hard

SONG:
sometimes it’s hard
to put a smile (on my face)
tear drops falling
melting the pain
it’s alright
to embrace
the pain.

the world moves fast
(but my heart stands still)
tears falling slow (against my will)
through the gray
i’ll find my lane—
it’s alright
to embrace
the pain.

waves crash soft
(on some lonesome shore)
whispers (of sorrow) knock
at my door.
the world chases sunlight
but i remain—
it’s alright
to embrace
the pain.

tear drops faling
mellting the pain
(and the thights cry too)
melting (the heartache)
i’ve clung to.
in the storm’s arms
i break my chain—
it’s alright
to embrace
the pain.

lights flicker, shadows grow—
emptiness speaks (of what i don’t know)
yet in the hollow
strength is gained—
it’s alright
to embrace
the pain.

sometimes it’s hard
to put a smile (on my face)
but tears are rivers
(time won’t erase).
let them fall
washing sorrow’s stain—
it’s alright
to embrace
the pain.
