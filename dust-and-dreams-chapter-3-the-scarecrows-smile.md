# Dust and Dreams – Chapter 3: The Scarecrow’s Smile

SONG COMMANDS (duet tongue lang portuguese):

Dust and Dreams – Chapter 3: The Scarecrow’s Smile
Styles: Pop, Violin, Kunqu, Steelpan, Marimba, Bass banjo, Horn harmonica, 12-string guitar, Fiddle, Hollow-body electric guitar, folk, soulful, harmonica, emo, violin, whistling

[Spoken Word Intro – Soft, poetic delivery with light ambient chords beneath]

Let’s learn some Portuguese vocabulary with a girl on a quest
Not from lessons or drills or a grammar test
But through fields of corn and a scarecrow’s jest
Where heart and wit put courage to rest

These aren’t just words—they're echoes and grain
Through rows of questions and gentle rain
Each line holds more than a language trace
It’s a walk through a world that rewrites space

So open your ears, let your feet be wise
We’re learning through laughter and wide open skies
From “milho” to “chapéu”, from “estrada” to “saber”
The yellow bricks teach more than they wear
