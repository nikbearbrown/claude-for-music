# There once was a cobbler, honest and true,

SONG:

"The Elves and the Shoemaker":

[Verse]
There once was a cobbler, honest and true,
With leather for one pair, he hadn’t a clue.
He cut it out neat, set it up on his stand,
And left it for morning, his tools in hand.

[Verse]
But when morning came, oh what a surprise!
A perfect pair sat there, right before his eyes.
Not a stitch out of place, each seam smooth and tight,
Made by hands unseen, in the dead of night.

[Chorus]
Oh, tiny little helpers, dancing out of sight,
Working through the darkness, until the morning light.
They stitch and sew with fingers quick,
And leave the cobbler’s shop so slick!

[Verse]
