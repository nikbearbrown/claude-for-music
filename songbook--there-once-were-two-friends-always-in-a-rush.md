# There once were two friends, always in a rush,

SONG:

There once were two friends, always in a rush,
Saying, "We'll catch up soon," in the midst of the hush.
Days turned to weeks, and weeks into years,
Their plans to "Catch Up" brought laughter and tears.

Let's not make plans to "Catch Up," my friend,
For time slips away in the end.
Let's savor moments while they're here,
Instead of waiting for another year.

They'd text and they'd call, but never quite meet,
Their lives full of chaos, a perpetual fleet.
"We'll catch up next month," they'd earnestly vow,
But somehow that time never seemed to allow.

Let's not make plans to "Catch Up," my friend,
For time slips away in the end.
Let's savor moments while they're here,
Instead of waiting for another year.

One day they woke up, their hair turned to gray,
Realizing those catch-ups had all slipped away.
Regrets filled their hearts for the times they had missed,
