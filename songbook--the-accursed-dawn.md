# The Accursed Dawn

SONG:

The Accursed Dawn
(A Mary Shelley Poem)

[Verse 1]
The letter came, with trembling hand,
A father’s grief upon the page.
No joyful welcome, no embrace,
But sorrow’s weight and wretched rage.

[Verse 2]
Oh, William! child of golden light,
Now cold beneath the weeping sky.
A voice once sweet, now stilled in death,
By hands unseen, condemned to die.
