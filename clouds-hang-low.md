# clouds (hang low)

SONG:
clouds (hang low)
heavy on my mind, rain
falls softly (gently) —
sometimes it's hard to (smile)
but sadness
is not a race—

waves whisper on shorelines,
crashing softly
sorrow: a secret
I cannot ignore—
in a world (chasing sun)
it's alright to be blue
the healing's begun—

rain falls on rooftops
melting pain—
in storm's quiet,
I find (peace) —
embracing the dark,
my soul (released) —

it’s alright
to feel this way
let the tears fall,
embrace the gray—
sometimes we need to let
our hearts break
in shadows,
strength we make—

lost echoes of tears,
melodies clear—
sadness whispers,
I am strong—
finding where
I belong—

through the rain,
I rise (above)
every tear, a testament of love—
in the storm,
I find my way
dancing through (rain)
each and every (day)

it’s okay to (be sad)
we all have (those days) —
let tears (fall down),
wash the pain away—
in darkness we (find) beauty—
never be ashamed
to be sad (today) —

candles burn low,
flickering light and shadow
in the stillness (I confess)
life feels hollow—
yet in this hollow,
a gentle strength I borrow
to face tomorrow’s light
with less sorrow—
