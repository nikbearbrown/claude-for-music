Ashputtel's Sorting Task

In a land of soot and sorrow,

Ashputtel,her name so sweet,
Worked so hard,she'd never cheat.

One day came a daunting chore,
A pile of lentils on the floor.
Mixed with ashes,dark and gray,
Sort them all,she heard them say.

Oh,Ashputtel,don't you fret,
There's a way to sort them yet.
With patience,work,and magic too,
You'll find the path to make it through.

To separate them easily.
