# When Morning Gilds the Skies


**Date/source note:** 1744

## Background

1744. From the rising of the sun to its going down the Lord's name is to be praised.
Psalm 113. From the rising of the sun to its going down the Lord's name is to be
praised. Explain Psalm 113. That's the theme behind this anonymous Catholic hymn,
Binefruin Morgan-Licht, which first appeared in the German hymn book, Cotolisha Gesson
book in 1744. It was translated into English a hundred years later by Edward Cazwald, a
Roman Catholic priest. Edward had grown up in an Anglican parsinage in Yatelye, England,
where his father was a church of England minister.

Following in his father's footsteps Edward became an Anglican curate in Stratford sub-
Casal Wiltshire. But in 1847 he converted to Catholicism. He delighted in translating
ancient hymns from Latin into English, and is the translator who gave a Saint Bernard's
Jesus the very thought of thee. In translating when morning guilds the skies he rendered
the verses freely and even added some of his own. Those who love this hymn as much as I
do, will be pleased to learn the original English version had 28-14 double stanzas. Here
are some new ones for you to sing.

When you begin the day, O never fail to say, May Jesus Christ be praised. And at your
work rejoice to sing with heart and voice, may Jesus Christ be praised. When air the
sweet church bell peels over hill and bell, may Jesus Christ be praised. O hark to what
it sings as joyously it rings, may Jesus Christ be praised. Be this it meals your grace
in every time and place, may Jesus Christ be praised. Be this when day is past of all
your thoughts the last, may Jesus Christ be praised. When birth from music longs this is
my song of songs, may Jesus Christ be praised.

When evening shadows fall this rings my curfew call, may Jesus Christ be praised. When
sleep her balm denies my silent spirit's eyes, may Jesus Christ be praised. When evil
thoughts molest with this I shield my breast, may Jesus Christ be praised. Sing sun and
stars of space, sing ye that see his face. Sing Jesus Christ be praised. Have this
feeling what you need to imply whole creation, or for I and ever more. Shall Jesus
Christ be praised?Guide me O thou great Jehovah! Guide me O thou great Jehovah! Pilgrim
through this barren land!

I am weak, but thou art mighty! Hold me with thy powerful hand! Bread of heaven, bread
of heaven! Feed me till I want no more! Feed me till I want no more!
