# The Road Through the Forest

---

## 1. Plain Lyrics (DistroKid)

She walks (& the yellow brick) road
Breaking beneath her steps—stumbling, yet steady—
The path (uneven), holes where she steps aside
Where he falls without mind to the ground
(no care at all)
They walk where yellow bricks break
Rough, uneven, each step they take
Scarecrow stumbles, but he doesn’t mind
With Dorothy there, they leave troubles behind
She laughs, picks him up, dusts him off

Fields grow (gray) in lonesome tones
Fewer trees, stones, just sky & empty earth—
They walk alone, where Toto jumps, she swerves
But he falls—twisting, curved
“no hunger here,” he says
“straw in my head—nothing to lose,”
(painted mouth, a blue eye)
“if i had a mouth to chew
My thoughts would spill, i’d come undone.”
They walk on, shadows bend in the sun

The trees close in, thick & slow
Sky disappears, (a darkened glow)
The land grows lonesome, no fences
To be found
The farther they go, the grayer the ground
She offers him bread, but he
Shakes his head
“no hunger, just straw instead.”
She talks of Kansas, gray & dry
“why leave Oz, beneath its vibrant sky?”

Her hands clutch this empty dream
As night folds around them
A cottage stands, hidden in green
They walk where yellow bricks break
Rough & uneven, each step they take
Scarecrow stumbles, yet doesn’t mind
With Dorothy, they leave troubles behind
They move (onward) through the night
Where steps break, trees close, shadows flow
The trees grow thick, light fades low

Darkness wraps the path they know
“i see a cottage,” Scarecrow says
“a bed of leaves to rest your head.”
She lies down, finds her sleep
As he stands guard, his watch to keep
So they move through night
Where yellow bricks break
Rough and uneven, each step they take
Scarecrow stumbles, yet he doesn’t mind
With Dorothy there, they leave troubles behind

& still they move
With dreams in her hands
While he stands with painted eyes
The straw beneath, the shadows flow
Through trees thick and paths unknown

## 4. Session Notes

**Key:** D minor
**Tempo:** Slow ballad ~68 BPM
**Time Signature:** 4/4
**Feel:** Mournful, sparse, building to cathartic release
**Style:** folk musical, acoustic country, storytelling pop, Chicago blues, gospel protest, soul

---

**Form Map:**

| Section | Bars (approx.) | Notes |
|---------|---------------|-------|
| Intro | 4 | Soft vocal warm-up with acoustic guitar establishing tone |
| Verse 1 | 8 | Sparse lead vocal, story-forward phrasing |
| Chorus | 8 | Harmonies enter, wider dynamics |
| Bridge | 4 | Textural shift and emotional lift |
| Outro | 4 | Fade with ambient tail and final refrain energy |

---

**Instrument List:**
- Acoustic Guitar: primary rhythm bed and song movement
- Violin: melodic countermelodies and emotional color
- Harmonica: melodic countermelodies and emotional color
- Electric Guitar: primary rhythm bed and song movement
- Organ: harmonic foundation and transitional fills

---

**Section-by-Section Notes:**

**Intro:** Keep the warm-up intimate and centered, with the lead instrument setting the harmonic bed.
**Verse 1:** Keep accompaniment sparse so the text stays intelligible; favor close, conversational vocal delivery.
**Chorus:** Add harmonies, broaden the stereo image, and let the rhythm section support the emotional peak.
**Bridge:** Shift texture with a held chord, solo response, or reduced percussion before returning to the main feel.
**Outro:** Let the final phrase breathe, then fade with reverb or natural ambience.

---

**Production Notes:**
Build the track around lyric clarity first, then add color in small layers. Keep the mix spacious enough for the vocal to carry the story, with the strongest dynamic lift reserved for the chorus or final refrain. Use the inferred style blend as a palette rather than a rigid genre box.
