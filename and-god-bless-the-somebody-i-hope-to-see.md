And God bless the somebody I hope to see.

Nik Bear vocalizes his prose as a daily prayer.

Vocalization, Lyrics, Arrangement, and Production: Nik Bear Brown

I see the moon, and it stares at me,
Reflecting all the places I’ve been, all I see—
L.A. palm trees, Northeastern breeze,
But I left my soul somewhere in between, unseen.
A shy boy lost in the city of dreams,
But she was real, her eyes, they gleamed.
She’s everything the books could never teach me,
Now I’m back in Boston, her light still haunts me.

Wait and pray, the night don’t fade away.
Will I ever see you again? I can’t say.
I let you go, but my heart won’t set free.
God bless the moon, and God bless me,
And God bless the somebody I hope to see.
