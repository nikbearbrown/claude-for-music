# Chapter 8: The Queen Beneath the Paws

---

## 1. Plain Lyrics (DistroKid)

We walked into woods where the shadows crept
And danger stirred where silence slept
A growl from the dark, a
Flash of fur
And suddenly no one dared to stir

A beast came near with a rumbling chest
Eyes like fire, no time to rest
But the lion, brave, with trembling paws
Stood up tall and roared with cause

He faced the beast, his mane held wide
Not to fight, but protect with pride
The others watched, their hearts so tight
As roars became the voice of right

The forest hushed, the danger passed
But the test had come and
Gone so fast
And just beyond the curling tree
A tiny voice spoke urgently

A mouse in a crown, so
Small, so sure
With a royal tone and heart so pure
“My people are trapped,” she softly pled
“Beneath the roots where the wild have fed”

So they knelt beside her silken throne
And promised she would not be alone
The lion bent down with mighty grace
To save the tiny, trembling race

He pulled and pawed, he cleared the way
The beasts were gone by end of day
And the Queen bowed low, her voice sincere
“You’ll have our help when need draws near”

They learned that strength is not just size
But measured in who lifts and tries
And even kings with tooth and claw
Can kneel before the smallest law

So onward went the heart and hoof
With every step, a living proof
That kindness rules through all it sees
Even whispered soft beneath the trees

## 4. Session Notes

**Key:** D minor
**Tempo:** Moderate ~84 BPM
**Time Signature:** 4/4
**Feel:** Mournful, sparse, building to cathartic release
**Style:** gospel, folk hymn, soul, Chicago blues, gospel protest, hip-hop

---

**Form Map:**

| Section | Bars (approx.) | Notes |
|---------|---------------|-------|
| Intro | 4 | Soft vocal warm-up with organ establishing tone |
| Verse 1 | 8 | Sparse lead vocal, story-forward phrasing |
| Chorus | 8 | Harmonies enter, wider dynamics |
| Bridge | 4 | Textural shift and emotional lift |
| Outro | 4 | Fade with ambient tail and final refrain energy |

---

**Instrument List:**
- Organ: harmonic foundation and transitional fills
- Choir: harmony support and chorus lift
- Acoustic Guitar: primary rhythm bed and song movement
- Electric Guitar: primary rhythm bed and song movement
- Handclaps: pulse, groove, and dynamic movement

---

**Section-by-Section Notes:**

**Intro:** Keep the warm-up intimate and centered, with the lead instrument setting the harmonic bed.
**Verse 1:** Keep accompaniment sparse so the text stays intelligible; favor close, conversational vocal delivery.
**Chorus:** Add harmonies, broaden the stereo image, and let the rhythm section support the emotional peak.
**Bridge:** Shift texture with a held chord, solo response, or reduced percussion before returning to the main feel.
**Outro:** Let the final phrase breathe, then fade with reverb or natural ambience.

---

**Production Notes:**
Build the track around lyric clarity first, then add color in small layers. Keep the mix spacious enough for the vocal to carry the story, with the strongest dynamic lift reserved for the chorus or final refrain. Use the inferred style blend as a palette rather than a rigid genre box.
