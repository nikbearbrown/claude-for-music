# Like a River Glorious


**Date/source note:** 1876

## Background

1876. I will extend peace to her like a river, Isaiah chapter 66, verse 12. In 1876
while vacationing in the south of Wales, Francis Havagall caught a severe cold and
suffered inflammation of the lungs. Told she might die, her response was, if I am really
going, it is good to be true. Her friends were amazed at how peacefully she faced the
prospect of dying. That same year she wrote to him, like a river glorious is God's
perfect peace over all victorious in its bright increase. Perfect yet it floweth, fuller
every day.

Perfect yet it groweth, deeper all the way. Stayed upon Jehovah, hearts are fully
blessed, finding as he promised, perfect peace and rest. Three years later while meeting
some boys to talk with them about the Lord, she ran into cold, wet weather and became
chilled. As her fever grew worse, her family became alarmed. It gradually became
apparent that Francis 42 was dying. On which Sunday, as one of her doctors left the
room, he said, goodbye, I shall not see you again. Then you really think I am going?
Asked Francis.

Yes. Today? Probably. Beautiful, said Francis. Too good to be true. Soon afterwards she
looked up smiling and said, splendid to be so near the gates of heaven. She asked her
brother to sing some hymns to her. Then he said to her, you have talked and written a
great deal about the king, and you will soon see him in his beauty. It's splendid, she
replied. I thought he would have left me here a long while, but he is so good that take
me now. A little later she whispered, come, Lord Jesus, come and fetch me. The terrible
rush of convulsion seized her, and when they ceased, the nurse gently laid her back on
her pillows.

Francis sister later wrote, then she looked up steadfastly as if she saw the Lord.
Surely nothing less heavenly could have reflected such a glorious radiance upon her
face. For ten minutes we watched that almost visible meeting with her king, and her
countenance was so glad, as if she were already talking to him. Then she tried to sing,
but after one sweet high note, heee! Her voice failed, and her brother commended her
soul into the Redeemer's hand
