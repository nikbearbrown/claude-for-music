# The Journey to the Great Oz

SONG:


The Journey to the Great Oz

they camp (beneath) a sky of trees
where stars drift close & shadows breathe
a fire crackles, (bread is broken)
they dream of Oz, their wishes spoken

Tin Man swings his axe through night
& Scarecrow, clumsy, gathers light
nuts to fill the basket tight,
while Lion wanders in search of bite

underneath those branches wide,
Tin Man chops as fire provides
Toto and Dorothy eat the last bread,
and the Lion declines, no bloodshed

morning breaks, and they find ahead
a ditch, so wide, where paths are led
a breath is held, Lion leaps across
one by one, over gap and loss

each leap a hope, a bound, a sun
and they cross the void, fears overrun
they walk the path where shadows lay
seeking warmth at the end of the day

deep woods again, and claws appear
kalidahs—wild beasts, the Lion’s fear
they cut the tree, so it will fall
and carry them across it all

Tin Man swings, and Scarecrow plots,
the tree is down, the beasts are caught
as the Lion roars, to halt the fight,
and fear holds ground beneath the night

then water waits, so they build a raft
small trees fall, the river laughs
as wood floats down to emerald dreams
where green fields lie, beneath sunbeams

through thick woods, where darkness creeps
kalidahs follow with claws to keep
a growl, a charge, as Lion stands,
while beasts advance across the lands

they cut away, and drop them down
to fall with snarls, their echoes drown

and now, the river ahead they meet
they build a raft, so small, so fleet
Tin Man swings, the Scarecrow lends,
while Dorothy eats as daylight bends

to cross, to sail toward the dawn
where Oz awaits, their hopes are drawn
they move beyond those silent sighs
where daylight waits, beneath the skies

beneath that sky where shadows lay,
their journey heads to Oz today
for heart, for courage, and for light
toward a land, dreams held tight
