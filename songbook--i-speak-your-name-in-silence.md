# i speak your name in silence,

SONG:

i speak your name in silence,
as moonlight weaves its fragile song,
you were always there, my queen,
but only now I see, where you belong.
your beauty like a quiet flame,
a glow I’d missed until today,
now you are the light that guides me,
in the night’s soft sway.

now I see you—
i was blind before,
but you were here, my Indian queen,
and so much more.
oh, you were close,
closer than the rain,
i was lost in shadows,
yet you remained.

my Indian queen, your beauty lingers—
like stars that light the darkest sky,
i see the depth within your gaze,
a truth that i can’t deny.
you were always standing there,
a breath within my lungs,
but now i feel your heartbeat,
in the song i’ve left unsung.

now I see you—
i was blind before,
but you were here, my Indian queen,
and so much more.
oh, you were close,
closer than the rain,
i was lost in shadows,
yet you remained.

i trace your form with fingers,
where once i could not see,
each glance unveils your splendor,
each smile, a new discovery.
you stood like hidden poetry,
unnoticed in the lines,
but now i find you shining,
your light, your love, divine.

my Indian queen, the breath i take,
the beauty in the unseen,
i was blinded by the commonplace,
but now i know you, my queen.
please, be my queen.
