# Let’s learn some Hindi vocabulary from a friendship deep and true

SONG:


 The Ramayana of Valmiki – Chapter 5: Rama’s Alliance with Sugriva and Hanuman
Styles: acoustic folk, gospel soul, bluegrass banjo, tabla, harmonica, spoken word, raspy vocals, humming, melodic rap, violin

[Spoken Word Intro – Female Voice, steady and uplifting with soft harmonium and banjo fingerpicking]
Let’s learn some Hindi vocabulary from a friendship deep and true
Where monkeys make pacts, and mountains move too
Not with textbooks or grammar drills
But through echoes in forests and the strength of wills

Words like “mitr” and “sahayata” bloom
From monkey cries to echoing gloom
Each name is sacred, each vow is flame
For even gods must call on names

[Vocal Warm-Up – Duet]

[Tongue Twister – Male Voice Spoken Word]
Sugriva sang solemn songs of sorrow
But Ram replied with right to borrow
Hanuman hailed hope for tomorrow

[Male Voice – Bold, rhythmic, banjo and tabla blend]
Ram walked alone beneath the sky
Till Hanuman came with a wondering eye
He bowed so low, his heart was clear
A servant of truth, a soul sincere

[Female Voice – Sweet and curious with humming]
In monkey form, so full of grace
Hanuman saw Ram’s noble face
He crossed the trees, he crossed his pride
To stand and serve by Rama’s side

[Male Voice – Steady and warm with harmonica fills]
Vanar raj Sugriva ran
Chhupa tha woh, bhay se tan
Ram ne kaha, “Main saath hoon”
“Apna dukh ab mera hoon”

[Female Voice – Bright and flowing with violin and banjo harmony]
A king betrayed, a brother’s blow
A promise made in forest glow
Ram struck down the one who lied
And Sugriva stood with strength and pride

[Together – Harmonized Duet, vibrant and resolute]
So hands were joined, a vow was sworn
To find the queen and end the scorn
From mountain tops to rivers wide
Ram, Hanuman, Sugriva—side by side
