# Dorothy and Toto Learn Hindi

SONG:

[Baritone Male Voice - Gentle and Lyrical]
Dorothy was a little ladki
Living on the wide maidan
With her chacha, Uncle Henry
And her chachi, Aunt Em

[Baritone Male Voice - Soft Learning Echo]
Ladki means girl
Kutta means dog
Ghar means house
On the prairie fog

[Baritone Male Voice - Storytelling Tone]
Their ghar was very chhota
With a roof and walls around
A table and some chairs
And bistar near the ground

[Baritone Male Voice - Bright and Curious]
Chhota means little
Bistar means bed
Darwaza means door
Where Dorothy stood and said

[Baritone Male Voice - Gentle Chorus]
Oh Dorothy and Toto
In the Kansas light
Learning Hindi words
Through the day and night
Ghar is house and kutta is dog
Aasmaan is sky above
Hawa is wind that starts to sing
And hansi means laugh with love

[Baritone Male Voice - Soft and Warm]
Outside was the maidan
Bhoora, wide, and dry
Not a tree or house was standing
Only aasmaan and sky

[Baritone Male Voice - Learning Echo]
Maidan means prairie
Aasmaan means sky
Bhoora means gray
Where the dry winds fly

[Baritone Male Voice - Playful Tone]
Toto was a kala kutta
With eyes so bright and small
He made Dorothy laugh and laugh
The happiest sound of all

[Baritone Male Voice - Gentle Learning Echo]
Kala means black
Kutta means dog
Hansi means laughter
Bright as morning fog

[Baritone Male Voice - Gentle Chorus]
Oh Dorothy and Toto
In the Kansas light
Learning Hindi words
Through the day and night
Ghar is house and kutta is dog
Aasmaan is sky above
Hawa is wind that starts to sing
And hansi means laugh with love

[Baritone Male Voice - Worried and Low]
One day the aasmaan turned darker
The hawa began to cry
Uncle Henry watched the clouds
And looked up at the sky

[Baritone Male Voice - Tense Learning Echo]
Hawa means wind
Toofan means storm
Darwaza means door
Where warnings form

[Baritone Male Voice - Storytelling Tone]
There's a cyclone coming, Em
Uncle Henry cried aloud
Run, Dorothy, run
Before the stormy cloud

[Baritone Male Voice - Quick and Rhythmic]
Daudna means run
Run to the door
Run to the cellar
Under the floor

[Baritone Male Voice - Dramatic and Gentle]
Toto hid below the bistar
Dorothy ran to see
She caught her little kutta
But the wind came wild and free

[Baritone Male Voice - Learning Echo]
Bistar means bed
Kutta means dog
Hawa means wind
In the stormy fog

[Baritone Male Voice - Rising Chorus]
Oh Dorothy and Toto
In the spinning light
Learning Hindi words
Through the windy night
Ghar is house and kutta is dog
Aasmaan is sky above
Hawa is wind that starts to roar
And hansi still means love

[Baritone Male Voice - Wondering Tone]
The ghar began to tremble
The ghar began to rise
It whirled around in circles
And floated to the skies

[Baritone Male Voice - Soft and Magical]
Dorothy sat quietly
Toto barked around
The house flew like a feather
Far above the ground

[Baritone Male Voice - Gentle Learning Echo]
Ghar means house
Aasmaan means sky
Chhota means little
As they floated high

[Baritone Male Voice - Calm and Sleepy]
The toofan howled around them
But Dorothy grew still
She waited for the morning
Over every field and hill

[Baritone Male Voice - Lullaby Tone]
She crawled across the swaying floor
To her little bistar near
Toto lay beside her
And she forgot her fear

[Baritone Male Voice - Soft Learning Echo]
Sona means sleep
Bistar means bed
Hawa means wind
Around her head

[Baritone Male Voice - Final Chorus]
Oh Dorothy and Toto
In the Kansas light
Learning Hindi words
Through the day and night
Ghar is house and kutta is dog
Aasmaan is sky above
Hawa is wind that sings and roars
And hansi means laugh with love

[Baritone Male Voice - Gentle Outro]
Ladki, kutta, ghar, and sky
Maidan wide and clouds up high
Toofan fades and dreams begin
Hindi words come floating in
