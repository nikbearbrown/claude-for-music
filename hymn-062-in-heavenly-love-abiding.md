# In Heavenly Love Abiding


**Date/source note:** 1850

## Background

1850, have I not commanded you? Be strong and of good courage, do not be afraid nor be
dismayed, for the Lord your God is with you wherever you go. Joshua 1. Verse 9 One of
our more reassuring hymns is Anna L. wearings in heavenly love abiding. Anna was born
into a Quaker family in Wales in 1823. As a teen she joined the Church of England and
was baptized in 1842. She was a lifelong student of the Hebrew language and daily read
from the book of Psalms in the original text. Anna's special burden was for those behind
bars, and she devoted herself to prison ministry and to causes like that discharged
prisoners aid society.

In 1850 she published a little volume of 19 hymns among them this one
