# A Wood’s Whisper

SONG:

A Wood’s Whisper

Adventures of Pinocchio I

[Verse 1]
Once in a woodshop, dusty and dim,
A log lay waiting, thick and grim,
Yet in its heart, a secret stirred,
A whisper faint, a voice unheard.

[Verse 2]
Mastro Cherry raised his hand,
To carve the wood as he had planned,
But just as the axe prepared its fall,
A childlike cry, a tiny call.

[Verse 3]
“Not so hard!” the wood implored,
And the old man paused, a bit unnerved.
He searched the shop, but all was still,
Save for the wood with a stubborn will.
