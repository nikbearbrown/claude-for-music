# When We All Get to Heaven


**Date/source note:** 1898

## Background

1898, and I will give you the keys of the kingdom of heaven, and whatever you bind on
earth will be bound in heaven, and whatever you lose on earth will be loosed in heaven.
Matthew chapter 16, verse 19. Eliza Edmunds-Sewitt was one of the premier women
hemwriters of the late 1800s and early 1900s. She wrote the popular hymn, Singing I Go
Along Life's Road, which was to have such a profound influence on soloist George Beverly
Shea. She is also the author of Will There Be Any Stars in My Crown?
