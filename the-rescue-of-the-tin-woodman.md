# The Rescue of the Tin Woodman

---

## 1. Plain Lyrics (DistroKid)

They found him—rusted, axe raised (high
A man (of tin) with a sigh
Frozen in shadows, alone
Dorothy (and scarecrow) standing close
Set him free (a yearning sigh)
Axe dropped low, he joins their stride
She runs quick with the oil-can
Frees his neck, his hands, his feet
And each limb, bound by rust
Now moving—his heart still incomplete

Morning light (she woke to find)
Toto chasing birds, air kind
They heard that groan, through trees and stone
There stood a figure, locked in time
He speaks of a love, of
Warmth he knew
Of a Munchkin girl lost from view
The witch’s curse, his heart did steal
And left him with only tin to feel
He says, “i’ve no heart, but

Still i seek
That love which beats—when all feels bleak.”
They walk, three shadows stretching long
Forest thickens, echoes strong
His axe swings wide (silver, gold)
Clearing a path, a tale retold
He spoke of love that slipped away
A curse by the witch, that fateful day
Each limb replaced by the tinsmith’s hand
The feeling lost, replaced by sand

“i need a heart,” he says,
Without a pulse
For the warmth i miss, in
The quiet of dusk
Scarecrow says, “my head’s dry, no brains inside
Yet i’ll seek Oz—my path to guide.”
Tin man sighs, “it’s love i seek,”
For life without heart leaves the spirit weak
They walk where trees grow thick, shadows play
Each step clearing a new way

For love is all, and all is dust
No heart, yet he must seek
Where love may rest
And down the road, the journey takes
Till green fields call and hearts awake
For brains, or hearts, or the
Dreams they sow
They walk together where shadows grow
And with his axe, he clears the way
Forest bends, yet does not sway

Three souls bound in silent trust
Walking through the trees, to where
Love finds its dust

## 4. Session Notes

**Key:** D minor
**Tempo:** ~92 BPM
**Time Signature:** 4/4
**Feel:** Mournful, sparse, building to cathartic release
**Style:** folk musical, acoustic country, storytelling pop, classical Persian, Sufi devotional, jazz ballad

---

**Form Map:**

| Section | Bars (approx.) | Notes |
|---------|---------------|-------|
| Intro | 4 | Soft vocal warm-up with acoustic guitar establishing tone |
| Verse 1 | 8 | Sparse lead vocal, story-forward phrasing |
| Chorus | 8 | Harmonies enter, wider dynamics |
| Bridge | 4 | Textural shift and emotional lift |
| Outro | 4 | Fade with ambient tail and final refrain energy |

---

**Instrument List:**
- Acoustic Guitar: primary rhythm bed and song movement
- Violin: melodic countermelodies and emotional color
- Harmonica: melodic countermelodies and emotional color
- Ney Flute: melodic countermelodies and emotional color
- Daf Drum: pulse, groove, and dynamic movement

---

**Section-by-Section Notes:**

**Intro:** Keep the warm-up intimate and centered, with the lead instrument setting the harmonic bed.
**Verse 1:** Keep accompaniment sparse so the text stays intelligible; favor close, conversational vocal delivery.
**Chorus:** Add harmonies, broaden the stereo image, and let the rhythm section support the emotional peak.
**Bridge:** Shift texture with a held chord, solo response, or reduced percussion before returning to the main feel.
**Outro:** Let the final phrase breathe, then fade with reverb or natural ambience.

---

**Production Notes:**
Build the track around lyric clarity first, then add color in small layers. Keep the mix spacious enough for the vocal to carry the story, with the strongest dynamic lift reserved for the chorus or final refrain. Use the inferred style blend as a palette rather than a rigid genre box.
