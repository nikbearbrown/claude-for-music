# Surely the darkness will cover me now

SONG:


Surely the darkness will cover me now
Night wraps the hills, the stars disavow
But even the shadows can't keep you away
The night turns to light when you stay

I thought I was hidden, I thought I was gone
Lost in the silence where no light shone
Yet you saw my heart through the midnight blue
For darkness is light unto you

Surely the darkness will hide me away
But your spirit whispers, I cannot stray
Even the night is bright in your view
And the light becomes night yet it shines with you
