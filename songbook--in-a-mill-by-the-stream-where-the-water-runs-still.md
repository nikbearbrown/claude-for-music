# In a mill by the stream, where the water runs still,

SONG:

In a mill by the stream, where the water runs still,
Lived a miller, his daughter, and a tale to thrill.
The miller boasted bold, “She spins straw into gold!”
So the king brought her in, to the palace of old.

The king locked her tight in a room full of straw,
“Spin this into gold by the dawn, or you’re gone.”
She sat with her tears, in despair and in fright,
Till a strange little man popped in late that night.

Rumpelstiltskin, with secrets and schemes,
Spinning wheels hum in shadowed dreams.
Gold out of straw, a pact you must pay,
Be careful the price, for debts come someday.

“What will you give me?” he asked with a grin,
“To turn all this straw to gold from within.”
“My necklace,” she whispered, and he took it with glee,
Spinning round, spinning gold, oh, so merrily.
