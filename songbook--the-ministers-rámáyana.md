# The Ministers (Rámáyana)

At Dasaratha’s side, they stand,
Wise sages with a guiding hand.
Vasishtha, Vámadeva too,
With wisdom vast and counsel true.
Their voices soft, their vision strong,
They keep the kingdom right from wrong.

In robes of light, eight lords surround,
Each bound by oaths, by honor crowned.
In battles bold, in peace they strive,
To keep the king’s great dreams alive.
Jayanta, bold, Vijay in might,
For war’s command, they guard the night.

In mercy’s name, in law’s embrace,
Dharmapál and Asok trace
A line of truth through wrong and right,
Each heart attuned to justice's sight.
Their gaze is firm, their judgments fair,
And every caste receives their care.

They know the land, each distant place,
As sunbeams touch the wide earth’s face.
Their minds, like rivers, steady flow
With secret truths they deeply know.
