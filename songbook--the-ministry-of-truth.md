# The Ministry of Truth

SONG:

The Ministry of Truth



THE PARTY TOLD YOU TO REJECT THE EVIDENCE OF YOUR EYES AND EARS
IT WAS THEIR FINAL, MOST ESSENTIAL COMMAND

They said,
“Truth is a tool, not a law
We shape it like clay, raw and flawed”
And you —
you blinked, and believed
your senses deceived
your heartbeat agreed.

They told you the sun sets in the east
and you nodded
though it rose behind your shoulder.

They burned the books,
then wrote new ones that claimed they never existed.
And when you reached for memory,
they handed you a mirror that only showed their face.

[Whistling gently under next verse – sorrowful]

They whispered:
"See? That man was always your enemy..."
though he fed you bread
and held your child
and kissed your mother's hand.

They rewrote love,
rewrote math,
rewrote the color of the sky.
They erased your name
and gave you a number that didn't belong to your body.

[Verse – rhythm building, finger snaps layered with soft bass pulse]

But what is a lie
if not a bruise on the truth
touched by silence
branded by proof?

You saw it
—you did—
you heard the scream
felt the tear in your skin
when they said
“That never happened.”

You said 2 + 2 = 4
They said
“Try again”
You said
“But I saw…”
They said
“You dreamed.”

[Bridge – quiet break, just acoustic guitar and soft hums]

And so you stopped speaking.
Started nodding.
Then you forgot how it sounded to say
"I know what I know."

[Outro – slow rise, gospel organ swelling]

But somewhere
beneath your ribs
a rebel memory drums:
