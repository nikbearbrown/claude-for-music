# I’m caught in this learning game,

SONG:

I’m caught in this learning game,
searching for balance between joy and pain,
they drive me insane,
seeking truth, but it’s hard to obtain.

Joy and Pain haunt my dreams,
no matter how hard I try, they won’t let me be—
drowning in facts, too much on my mind,
but baby, I’ll find a way, just give me time.

Learning to love Pain and Joy,
letting go, I take my stance,
in this dance of emotions,
I’ll find my own advance.

In the depths of my soul, I seek truth,
through ebb and flow, error and proof,
Pain whispers softly in the night,
while Joy dances in the moon’s pale light.
