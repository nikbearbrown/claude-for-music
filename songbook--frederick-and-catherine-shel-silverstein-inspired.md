# Frederick and Catherine (Shel Silverstein-inspired)

SONG:

Frederick and Catherine (Shel Silverstein-inspired)

Frederick said, “Now listen, Kate,
When I’m back, I’ll want a plate.
A steak, some ale, a dinner fine,”
“Oh sure,” said Kate, “it’ll be just divine!”

Kate’s heart is big, her plans are grand,
She does the best with what’s on hand.
Sometimes things go quite astray,
But Kate, she finds another way!
