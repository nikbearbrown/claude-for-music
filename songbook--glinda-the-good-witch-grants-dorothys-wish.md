# Glinda The Good Witch Grants Dorothy’s Wish

SONG:

Glinda The Good Witch Grants Dorothy’s Wish

Before they could see Glinda, they cleaned up nice,
Dorothy washed her face, Scarecrow shaped just right.
The Tin Woodman polished, and Lion shook his mane,
Ready for Glinda, they went to make their claim.

Glinda, the Good, sat upon her ruby throne,
With hair like fire and eyes of calm blue stone.
She listened to their story, full of magic and strife,
And asked Dorothy, "What do you want in life?"

Dorothy told her tale, from Kansas through the storm,
Her friends, her journey, the love she'd now worn.
She wished to go home, to the fields so wide,
To Aunt Em, before she wept and cried.

Glinda smiled, with a kiss on Dorothy’s cheek,
“I can help you, child, your home you’ll seek.
But give me the Golden Cap, if you will,
I’ll use it just three times to serve my fill.”
