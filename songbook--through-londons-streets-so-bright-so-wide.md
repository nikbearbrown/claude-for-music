# Through London’s streets, so bright, so wide,

SONG:

[Verse]
Through London’s streets, so bright, so wide,
I walked in shadow, lost inside.
A city vast, of art and lore,
Yet all its light could heal no more.

[Verse]
Clerval, joyous, keen of mind,
Sought wisdom in mankind refined.
Yet I, a prisoner of my fate,
Saw only grief accumulate.

[Verse]
Each face I met, so free, so bright,
Reflected back my soul’s own blight.
For bound to horror, dread, and pain,
I walked this earth, a ghost in chain.

[Verse]
Through Oxford’s spires and Windsor’s green,
Past rivers deep and valleys seen,
Yet in each place, no solace found,
Only echoes, loss profound.

[Verse]
