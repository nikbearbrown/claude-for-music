# Once a girl, as sweet as could be,

SONG:

Once a girl, as sweet as could be,
Wore a hood, red as cherries on a tree.
Through the woods with cake and wine,
To her dear grandma—heart so kind.

Little Red Riding Hood, so bold, so free,
Stay on the path, as safe as can be.
But wolves are clever, sly, and mean—
Not all’s as gentle as it seems.

A wolf with hunger in his eye,
Spied Red Riding Hood as she walked by.
“Good day, dear child,” he grinned and said,
With acts of smelly plans in his head.

“Flowers are pretty, see them grow!
Pick some for Grandma before you go.”
And Red Riding Hood smiled, off the trail she ran,
Gathering blooms by wolf’s cruel plan.

To Grandma’s house, the wolf sped fast,
With hunger strong, he got there at last.
One big gulp, and Grandma was gone—
