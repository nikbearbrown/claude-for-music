# Dust and Dreams – Chapter 7: Sparks and Golden Screws

SONG COMMANDS (duet lang portuguese):

Dust and Dreams – Chapter 7: Sparks and Golden Screws
Styles: &B, Poetry, Dembow Beat, Chicago Rap, Gospel Organ, Acoustic guitar, Lap steel guitar, Tremolo harmonica, Open-back banjo, Baroque violin, folk, soulful, whistling

[Spoken Word Intro – Soft, poetic delivery with light ambient chords beneath]

Let’s learn some Portuguese vocabulary in the shimmer of green
Where hope has a gate and doubt’s unseen
Not all doors need locks, not all paths need keys
Sometimes belief is what truly frees

This isn’t a lesson with borders or books
But one that lives in glances and looks
From “cidade” to “portão”, from “luz” to “ver”
The journey keeps turning with new words to share

So we step through silence, through shimmer and shine
Where the smallest screw holds the grandest design
For courage and magic are built the same way—
With hearts that keep walking, come night or day

Let’s learn some words.
Vamos aprender algumas palavras.

English	Portuguese

Gate is	Portão
Glasses is	Óculos
Green is	Verde
Wizard is	Mágico
Truth is	Verdade
Light is	Luz
Hope is	Esperança
Wall is	Muro
Friend is	Amigo / Amiga
Step is	Passo
Dream is	Sonho
Walk is	Caminhar
Open is	Abrir
