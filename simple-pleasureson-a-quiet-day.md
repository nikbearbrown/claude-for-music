# simple pleasures—on a quiet day

---

## 1. Plain Lyrics (DistroKid)

Fresh cookies (baking) fill the room
With warmth & a treat
My favorite hoodie wrapping me
In the quiet complete—
The ladybug strolls on leaves (so green)
A tiny gem, unseen but lovely
And with each step it sings
Helping flowers bloom year after year
Simple pleasures—on a quiet day
In smallest things (i find my way)

Through each moment (soft and slow)
The beauty of life begins to show
Doodling (my mind roams free)
Music in the background, like coming home
A text arrives (from someone special)
& rain’s scent drifts quietly through the room
The caterpillar spins its cocoon
To rest in warmth (of the noon)—
When the time is right, it
Will break free

And as a moth (it will be)
Passing dandelions as they blossom bright
Golden stars in morning light—
When their flowers turn to white
They dance in the breezes light
Watching (my favorite bollywood) with no rush
Sitting in silence, no need for a hush
The peace settles in like rain
On the earth
These little joys reminding me of my worth

The smell of fresh laundry (warm
From the dryer)
Comfort fills the room, lifting me higher
I fold each piece (slowly, savoring the touch)
And in these quiet tasks, i
Find so much
The breeze (a soft whisper) through
The window’s crack
Curtains sway gently (no rush, no lack)
In the rhythm of the day

There’s a grace—
A slower pace that fills this space
Flipping through an old journal (the pages thin)
Words from the past, thoughts begin—
Revisiting places i've already been
But peace now settles (like a quiet within)
The sparrow chirps from a tree so tall
With cheerful song (a call for all)
It flits to a dandelion’s head
A fluffy wish—a dream to spread

A small plant (stretching) on the windowsill
Growing day by day, quietly still
Even in stillness there’s movement (to see)
And in those moments, i feel free
The warmth of socks (fresh from the drawer)
Wrapping around my feet like more—
In the simple act of care, i find
A joy so rare, a peace of mind

## 4. Session Notes

**Key:** G major
**Tempo:** ~92 BPM
**Time Signature:** 4/4
**Feel:** Warm, narrative, gradually building with a singable refrain
**Style:** children's folk, lullaby, playful pop, Chicago blues, gospel protest, soul

---

**Form Map:**

| Section | Bars (approx.) | Notes |
|---------|---------------|-------|
| Intro | 4 | Soft vocal warm-up with ukulele establishing tone |
| Verse 1 | 8 | Sparse lead vocal, story-forward phrasing |
| Chorus | 8 | Harmonies enter, wider dynamics |
| Bridge | 4 | Textural shift and emotional lift |
| Outro | 4 | Fade with ambient tail and final refrain energy |

---

**Instrument List:**
- Ukulele: primary rhythm bed and song movement
- Piano: harmonic foundation and transitional fills
- Handclaps: pulse, groove, and dynamic movement
- Electric Guitar: primary rhythm bed and song movement
- Organ: harmonic foundation and transitional fills

---

**Section-by-Section Notes:**

**Intro:** Keep the warm-up intimate and centered, with the lead instrument setting the harmonic bed.
**Verse 1:** Keep accompaniment sparse so the text stays intelligible; favor close, conversational vocal delivery.
**Chorus:** Add harmonies, broaden the stereo image, and let the rhythm section support the emotional peak.
**Bridge:** Shift texture with a held chord, solo response, or reduced percussion before returning to the main feel.
**Outro:** Let the final phrase breathe, then fade with reverb or natural ambience.

---

**Production Notes:**
Build the track around lyric clarity first, then add color in small layers. Keep the mix spacious enough for the vocal to carry the story, with the strongest dynamic lift reserved for the chorus or final refrain. Use the inferred style blend as a palette rather than a rigid genre box.
