# She locked herself high, no door to the light,

SONG:

She locked herself high, no door to the light,
a tower of dreams, a cage built tight.
But she let down her hair for a love that’s rare,
and freedom was found in the courage to care.

Ashputtel (Cinderella)
Ashes to riches, dirt on her hands,
but dreams don’t die, like buried plans.
She rose through the dark, found her way to the ball,
and danced with hope, though they wanted her to fall.

Little Red-Cap (Little Red Riding Hood)
Little Red walks where the shadows creep,
wolves in the woods where danger sleeps.
But with eyes wide open, she didn’t stray,
turning fear into fire, she found her way.
