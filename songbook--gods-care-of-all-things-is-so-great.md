# God’s care of all things is so great,

SONG:

God’s care of all things is so great,
His love for us is sure and true,
He watches over all we do,
And guards us early, guards us late.

Thanks for the blessings of each day,
Thanks for the work, thanks for the play,
Thanks for the love that makes us gay,
Thanks for everything.

God bless the work we try to do,
God bless the work for me and you,
God bless the hands that work so true,
God bless the work we do.

Thanks for Thy constant care, O Lord,
Thanks for Thy love and truth and word,
Thanks for the work by which we live,
Thanks for the blessings Thou dost give.

Summer comes with flowers and song,
Winter days are cold and long,
Summer comes with sun and cheer,
Winter’s snow is cold and drear.
