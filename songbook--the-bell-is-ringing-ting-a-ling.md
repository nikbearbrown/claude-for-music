# The bell is ringing, ting-a-ling,

SONG:

The bell is ringing, ting-a-ling,
Her brown eyes glow, like autumn’s gold.
A glance from her can calm the sea,
Her beauty’s all I ever see.

Her skin, like dusk, warm and divine,
A softness where the stars align.
Each step she takes, the earth sings too,
I’m drawn to her, she never knew.
