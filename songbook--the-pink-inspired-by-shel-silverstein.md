# The Pink (Inspired by Shel Silverstein)

SONG:
The Pink (Inspired by Shel Silverstein)

Once a queen wished on the morning light
For a child who could wish with all his might.
And soon she had her heart’s delight—
A boy who’d bring her dreams to life.

The cook, he stole this child away,
Left blood and feathers where he lay,
Blamed the queen and locked her tight,
In a tower that never saw the light.

But angels came and kept her fed,
As seven years of night she led.
And her son, with wishes deep and true,
Found his way back to renew.

He wished his friend a pink so fair,
A flower he could hold with care.
Then to the tower he climbed so high,
And freed his mother with a sigh.
