# Rejoice, Ye Pure in Heart


**Date/source note:** 1865

## Background

1865. Rejoice in the Lord always. Again I will say, rejoice. Philippians chapter 4 verse
4. Edward H. Plumptree was a Christian intellectual. Born in London in 1821, he became a
well-known Anglican scholar, author, theologian, and preacher. For many years he served
as Chaplain of King's College, where he also taught pastoral theology and New Testament
exegesis. He wrote books on the classics, history, theology, biblical criticism, and
biography. He served on the Old Testament Company for the revision of the authorized
version of the Holy Scriptures, and for the last ten years of his life, 1881 to 1891, he
was the Dean of Wells Cathedral.

Edward was also a poet. In May 1865, he was preparing for the annual choir festival in
the majestic towering cathedral of Peterborough, England, needing a long and celebratory
processional to give the participating choir's time to proceed down the aisles. He
focused his thoughts on two passages of Scripture. Psalm 20 verse 5. We will rejoice in
your salvation, and in the name of our God we will set up our banners. Philippians
chapter 4 verse 4. Rejoice in the Lord always. Again I will say, rejoice. Inspired by
those verses, Edward 44, began writing.

Rejoice, he pure in heart. Rejoice, give thanks and sing. Your glorious banner wave on
high. The cross of Christ, your king. Rejoice. Rejoice. Rejoice. Give thanks and sing.
Edward composed eleven stanzas in all. Most Temnals today use only stanzas 1, 2, 7, and
10. Here are some of the omitted verses. Yes, onward, onward still with him and chant
and song. Through gate and porch and columned aisle. The hallowed pathways throng.
Rejoice. Rejoice. Rejoice, give thanks and sing. Your clear Hosanna's rays and allelus
is loud, whilst answering echoes upward float, like wreaths of incense cloud.

Rejoice. Rejoice. Rejoice, give thanks and sing. Praise him who reigns on high, the Lord
whom we adore. The Father, Son, and Holy Ghost. One God for evermore. Rejoice. Rejoice.
Rejoice. Give thanks and sing
