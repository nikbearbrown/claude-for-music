# rock-a-bye,

SONG:

rock-a-bye,
(baby on
the treetop)
when the wind
blows
the cradle
will rock
if the bough breaks
the cradle falls
but daddy
(will catch)
cradle and all
baby is drowsing
cozy & fair
daddy sits
near, in his
rocking chair
forward
           & back
      he swings
and though (baby sleeps)
he hears
what daddy sings
hush-a-bye baby
up in the sky
on a soft cloud—
it’s easy to fly
angels keep watch
over as
you sleep
so hush-a-bye baby
don’t make a peep
rock-a-bye baby,
don’t fear
never-mind (baby)
daddy is near
wee little fingers,
eyes shut tight
now sound asleep,
(until morning light)
