# The Little Peasant (Shel Silverstein-inspired)

SONG:

The Little Peasant (Shel Silverstein-inspired)

Once there was a peasant small,
In a village rich and tall.
Dreams he had, though coins were few,
To own a cow, to see it through.

Oh, dreams may dance on simple ground,
From little hopes, great joys are found.
A wooden calf, a plan so fine—
Sometimes truth walks a crooked line.
