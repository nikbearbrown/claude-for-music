# In my mind, I see her face,

SONG:

In my mind, I see her face,
The one I never knew, lost in space.
I ask the  leaves so green,
“How do you heal when longing’s unseen?”
She smiles, strolls, with grace so light,
“I help the flowers bloom, year after year, through day and night.”

[Chorus]
I wait, I pray, beneath these skies,
Wondering if longing ever truly dies.
