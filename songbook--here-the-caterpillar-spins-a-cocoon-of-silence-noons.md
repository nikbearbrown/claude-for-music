# here the caterpillar (spins) a cocoon of (silence) noon’s

SONG:

here the caterpillar (spins) a cocoon of (silence) noon’s
warmth cradles its hidden (flight) for the day it becomes a moth of light
The caterpillar spins its cocoon,
To rest in the warmth of the noon,
When time is right, it will break free,
And as a moth, it will be.
and the dandelion bright with (stars) a gold in morning’s soft-spoken (glow) grows (whispered) white, to dance with breezes free and slight
