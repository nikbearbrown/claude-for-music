# The Trial of the Innocent

SONG:

The Trial of the Innocent
(A Mary Wollstonecraft Shelley Poem)

[Verse 1]
The bells did toll the hour grim,
Where justice wore a hollow guise.
A shadow loomed in court so dim,
And silent tears filled weary eyes.

[Verse 2]
She stood, adorned in mourning’s grace,
Yet scorn had marred her tender frame.
Her tranquil brow, her saintly face,
Were now but masks for whispered blame.
