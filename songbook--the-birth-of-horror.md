# The Birth of Horror

SONG:

The Birth of Horror
(A Mary Shelley Poem)

[Verse 1]
A dreary night, the storm did wail,
Rain wept upon the window’s veil.
A flickering light, a breath, a sigh,
A yellowed eye, a life drawn nigh.

[Verse 2]
O Maker’s hands, so frail, so bold,
To sculpt from death, to challenge old.
Yet beauty mocked in ghastly skin,
