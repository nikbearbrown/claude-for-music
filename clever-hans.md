# CLEVER HANS

SONG:

CLEVER HANS

[Verse]
Hans with a rope, he drags a bacon treat,
The dogs come by, it’s gone in a beat.
Mother frowns and shakes her head,
"Carry it next time, Hans," she said.

[Chorus]
"Oh, clever Hans, clever Hans," they say,
"Will he ever learn the proper way?"
He shrugs and smiles, as his mother sighs,
"Never mind, I'll do better next time!"

Mother sighs, "Hans, that's not how it goes."
