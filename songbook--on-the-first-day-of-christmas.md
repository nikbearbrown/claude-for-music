# On the first day of Christmas,

SONG:


On the first day of Christmas,
My beloved gave to me,
A partridge in a pear tree.

On the second day of Christmas,
My beloved gave to me,
Two turtle doves,
And a partridge in a pear tree.

On the third day of Christmas,
My beloved gave to me,
Three French hens,
Two turtle doves,
And a partridge in a pear tree.

On the fourth day of Christmas,
My beloved gave to me,
Four birds softly calling,
Three French hens,
Two turtle doves,
And a partridge in a pear tree.

On the fifth day of Christmas,
My beloved gave to me,
Five golden bands,
Four birds softly calling,
Three French hens,
Two turtle doves,
And a partridge in a pear tree.

On the sixth day of Christmas,
My beloved gave to me,
Six geese a-laying,
Five golden bands,
Four birds softly calling,
Three French hens,
Two turtle doves,
And a partridge in a pear tree.

On the seventh day of Christmas,
My beloved gave to me,
Seven swans in water gliding,
Six geese a-laying,
Five golden bands,
Four birds softly calling,
Three French hens,
Two turtle doves,
And a partridge in a pear tree.

On the eighth day of Christmas,
My beloved gave to me,
Eight maids a-milking,
Seven swans in water gliding,
Six geese a-laying,
Five golden bands,
Four birds softly calling,
Three French hens,
Two turtle doves,
And a partridge in a pear tree.

On the ninth day of Christmas,
My beloved gave to me,
Nine ladies dancing,
Eight maids a-milking,
Seven swans in water gliding,
Six geese a-laying,
Five golden bands,
Four birds softly calling,
Three French hens,
Two turtle doves,
And a partridge in a pear tree.

On the tenth day of Christmas,
My beloved gave to me,
Ten lords leaping high,
Nine ladies dancing,
Eight maids a-milking,
Seven swans in water gliding,
Six geese a-laying,
Five golden bands,
Four birds softly calling,
Three French hens,
Two turtle doves,
And a partridge in a pear tree.

On the eleventh day of Christmas,
My beloved gave to me,
Eleven pipers piping,
Ten lords leaping high,
Nine ladies dancing,
Eight maids a-milking,
Seven swans in water gliding,
Six geese a-laying,
Five golden bands,
Four birds softly calling,
Three French hens,
Two turtle doves,
And a partridge in a pear tree.

On the twelfth day of Christmas,
My beloved gave to me,
Twelve drummers drumming,
Eleven pipers piping,
Ten lords leaping high,
Nine ladies dancing,
Eight maids a-milking,
Seven swans in water gliding,
Six geese a-laying,
Five golden bands,
Four birds softly calling,
Three French hens,
Two turtle doves,
And a partridge in a pear tree.
