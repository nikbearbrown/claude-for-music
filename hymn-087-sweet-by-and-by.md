# Sweet By and By


**Date/source note:** 1868

## Background

1868. In my father's house are many mansions. If it were not so, I would have told you.
I go to prepare a place for you. John chapter 14 verse 2. In 1868 a pharmacist named
Sandford Fillmore Bennett, 31, was filling prescriptions and handling sales at his
apothecary in Elcorn, Wisconsin. His friend Joseph Webster entered the store. Joseph was
a local musician, vocalist, violinist, and amateur composer, who suffered from periods
of depression. The two men had occasionally collaborated on hymns and songs, Sandford
writing the words and Joseph the music.

On this particular day Joseph was unusually blue in his face was long, looking up
Sandford asked, what is the matter now? It's no matter, Joseph replied, it will be all
right by and by. An idea for a hymn hit Sandford like a flash of sunlight. Sitting at
his desk, he began writing as fast as he could. The words came almost instantly. Two
customers entered the drugstore, but no attempt was made to assist them. Sandford was
too absorbed in his poem, so they salied over to the stove and visited with Joseph.
Finally Sandford rose and joined them, handing a sheet of paper to his friend.

Here is your prescription, Joe, he said, I hope it works. Webster read the words aloud.
There's a land that is fairer than day, and by faith we can see it afar. For the father
waits over the way to prepare us a dwelling place there. In the sweet buy and buy we
shall meet on that beautiful shore. Instantly, a tune suggested itself, and Joseph
jotted down some notes. Picking up his fiddle, he played his melody over a timer too,
then said to the others, we for make a good male quartet. Let's try the new song and see
how it sounds.

A sweet buy and buy was being sung for the first time another customer, our our crossby,
entered the store. Jettelman, he said, I never heard that song before, but it is
immortal. He was right. For over a hundred years we've been singing an immortal hymn
that was written in less than thirty minutes in a drugstore
